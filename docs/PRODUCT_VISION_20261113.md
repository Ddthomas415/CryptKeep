# CRYPTKEEP — PERMANENT PRODUCT VISION

CryptKeep has TWO equally important long-term objectives:

1. Become a reliable, risk-controlled automated cryptocurrency trading system.
2. Become an intelligent trading research platform capable of discovering, testing, evaluating, selecting and monitoring trading strategies.

Neither objective replaces the other.

The trading engine executes validated strategies.

The intelligence engine researches opportunities, develops hypotheses, conducts experiments and identifies potentially better strategies.

The feedback engine compares expected and actual results and identifies deterioration or opportunities for improvement.

Development principles:

- Preserve existing owner-approved tasks.
- Do not silently park or cancel work.
- Do not confuse the current strategy with the long-term product.
- Do not build AI capabilities without a clear purpose and measurable value.
- Prioritize reliable data, reproducible research and realistic execution.
- Keep research experiments isolated from live trading.
- Never allow autonomous research to authorize its own live deployment.
- Maintain independent validation and explicit risk controls.
- Avoid endless planning and repetitive task reprioritization.

Every new capability must explain how it contributes to one of the two objectives.

The goal is not simply to build a trading bot.

The goal is to build an intelligent trading platform that can discover opportunities, establish whether they are real, select the strongest candidates and execute them reliably under controlled risk.

This vision does not authorize changes to existing priorities, scope, security restrictions or live-trading permissions.
