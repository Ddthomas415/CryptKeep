# CMC primary analysis: prospective timing amendment

Role: DIRECTOR (proposal); GATE (recorded review). Risk: HIGH.
Status: ACCEPTED_WITH_RISK for prospective timing only. Separate Hooke review
found no blockers and verified the date arithmetic and preservation boundaries;
the user approved the amendment. This is not statistical certification.
User authorized the recommended project treatment. This amendment fixes timing,
not a confidence method, trading rule, scheduler or scoring permission.

## Fixed window and one primary look

- Required prior-history collection: October 11 through November 9, 2026 UTC.
- Primary entry dates: November 10, 2026 through February 7, 2027 inclusive.
- Entry-window bounds: [2026-11-10T00:00:00Z, 2027-02-08T00:00:00Z).
- Ninety calendar entry days, including valid observed zero-event days. Events
  are not required on every date; dates must not be selected by returns.
- Earliest primary analysis: February 15, 2027 at 12:00 UTC. This conservatively
  allows seven-day endpoints and the existing twelve-hour endpoint tolerance
  for the final entry date to finish. This is a not-before boundary, not a
  promise that sufficient evidence or a validated method will exist then.

The prospective start avoids using known historical outcomes to choose a window.
All earlier observations and original artifacts remain available as diagnostics;
they are not silently discarded or represented as fresh primary confirmation.
The existing 90-day collection floor remains necessary but is not sufficient.

## Coverage and refusal

Require the accepted preceding-30-UTC-day coverage rule for each first appearance,
valid list observations throughout the declared entry window, resolved eligible
membership, and qualified mature endpoints for every eligible event. Known entry
rule exclusions retain their explicit reasons. Unknown membership and missing
exit outcomes cannot become exclusions or zero-event days.

A missing day does not restart or shift the window. The declared study concludes
INCOMPLETE if coverage cannot be established from contemporaneously captured
evidence. Late retrieval cannot manufacture an absent historical trending list.
Zero eligible events, inadequate power, or unsupported statistical assumptions
also cannot produce a positive result. Ninety days is not a power guarantee.

## No success-driven repeated looks

Before the not-before boundary, report collection health, identity, coverage and
endpoint availability only; no primary return-based success screening. Existing
historical descriptive reports remain preserved. Do not promote on partial data.

Allow one primary economic evaluation under a separately preregistered,
independently reviewed market-applicable statistical contract, including its
target, assumptions, multiplicity and refusal rules. If no such contract exists,
report the study as INCOMPLETE rather than using V4 or diagnostic bands as proof.
Do not inspect outcomes first and select a method afterward. Further windows or
looks require a separate prospective amendment, not repeated testing until pass.

The event-weighted seven-day net excess versus matched BTC, fees, identity,
entry/exit quote rules and existing all-ranges-positive condition are unchanged.
This document alone cannot enable scoring or amend executable gates. No campaign,
account, schedule, collector, credential or original artifact is changed.
