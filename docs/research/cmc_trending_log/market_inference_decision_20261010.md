# Market inference: decision boundary

Role: DIRECTOR. Risk: HIGH. Status: INCOMPLETE; no method selected or implemented.

## Recommendation

Keep the existing event-weighted seven-day net excess target and frozen trading
rules. Continue forward collection and descriptive diagnostics. Do not claim
nominal market confidence from the finite-family V4 reference or replace the
primary target with a daily mean. No further synthetic search is authorized by
this memo.

## Inputs a defensible contract must justify

| Input | Current boundary |
|---|---|
| Eligible population | Identity-, quote-, timing- and history-qualified first appearances under existing rules |
| Dependence and regime changes | No market-supported dependence envelope is established yet |
| Scale and tails | No justified market tail/scale bound is established; observed maxima are not population bounds |
| Missing outcomes | Report coverage and missingness; do not equate available cases with the full eligible population |
| Confidence claim | Specify event-weighted target and whether inference is conditional or population-wide |
| Repeated looks | No early primary scoring; any future inference contract must bind permitted looks and multiplicity |
| Refusal | Unsupported assumptions, insufficient eligible coverage or invalid evidence must yield INCOMPLETE, not a positive verdict |

Minimum event/day counts are necessary planning inputs, not evidence that these
assumptions hold. Clipping returns would change the target and requires an
explicit amendment, not silent implementation. Neither a profitable historical
path nor a passing synthetic test establishes the missing market assumptions.

The highest-leverage next evidence is qualified forward coverage and a defensible
assumption proposal, reviewed before new validation. This memo does not change
the existing all-ranges-above-zero gate, fees, observations, accounts or schedules.
