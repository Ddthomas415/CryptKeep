### 1. "Can AI Make Money in Crypto? Measuring the Gap from Backtests to Real Markets" (Yu, Zhou, Ding et al.)
- **Primary URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34510v1
- **Date and type:** 2026-09-28, preprint (multi-institution). Read by two sub-scouts independently, ~85% of full text via fetch summary; the main scout's own search did not surface it in the index, so the URL is from sub-scout fetches.
- **Summary:** 32 methods (gradient-boosted trees, LSTM/TCN/GAT, RL agents, LLM and multi-agent frameworks) plus buy-and-hold on 10 large-cap coins on OKX, with configurations frozen after 2024 validation, then evaluated three ways: a 2025 backtest, exchange-hosted paper trading (Jun-Sep 2026) and small real-money live trading (Aug-Sep 2026). The point is the gap: methods that look profitable in a backtest largely stop being profitable once they face real order handling, latency and the forward market.
- **Claimed effect:** 23 of 24 ML methods profitable in the 2025 backtest; only 2 of 8 ML methods profitable in paper trading; median ML return fell from +36.0% (backtest) to -9.8% (paper). Mean absolute backtest-vs-paper gap over the same window 6.25 pp return, 14.05 pp alpha. Mean decision latency ~19 s (ML), ~187 s (LLM), ~523 s (agents). Fees 10 bps spot, 5 bps perps.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** short forward windows (paper ~4 months, live ~4 weeks) give low power; large caps only; live includes perp shorts; sub-scouts noted internal inconsistencies (top 4 vs top 5 methods taken live).
- **Risks:** low power; method selection for live stage; perps not available to the operator.
- **Data, cost, licence:** reading only; free.
- **Repo relation:** supports the repo's existing paper-forward stage (`paper_btc_eth_trend_v2_20261012/`) and the survey's exclusion of ML direction models (line 56). No repo hit for this paper.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** read the full paper and record, in a short note, whether any CryptKeep promotion gate should require a minimum forward paper period for any model-based signal; no market data.

### 2. Cakici, Shahzad, Będowska-Sójka, Zaremba, "Machine learning and the cross-section of cryptocurrency returns" (with Fieberg, Liedtke, Zaremba, "Cryptocurrency anomalies and economic constraints")
- **Primary URL:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finana/v94y2024ics1057521924001765.html (open-access full text: https://open.icm.edu.pl/bitstreams/43a03fcf-3a43-4262-8618-0d0db9d158dc/download; companion: https://open.icm.edu.pl/items/2350d455-72e4-4845-a442-9c4024be7fb6/full)
- **Date and type:** International Review of Financial Analysis 94 (2024), peer-reviewed. Cakici et al. full text read via fetch summary; Fieberg et al. abstract only.
- **Summary:** OLS, PLS, LASSO, elastic net, random forest, boosted trees, a small neural net and an equal-weight model combination on 40 coin characteristics for 552 coins, weekly expanding-window re-estimation, quintile long-short with a one-day skip. Simple models match complex ones; signal comes mainly from price level, past alpha, illiquidity and momentum, and the long leg carries much of the alpha. The companion paper argues that size/volume anomalies live in economically negligible microcaps and that momentum alpha comes mostly from shorts.
- **Claimed effect:** Jul 2017-Jan 2023 (OOS from Mar 2018). Combined-model value-weighted long-short 2.37%/week, Sharpe 1.66 gross; 0.34%-1.99%/week net of 30/40 bps one-way; spread-based costs remove ~57% of gross profit; breakeven costs 45-192 bps; weekly turnover 40-70%.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** the authors' own checks: excluding the smallest coins or adding price filters cuts profits; two-week holding cuts alpha ~45%; most of 4,923 alternative designs underperform the baseline (a sign of specification sensitivity). Fieberg et al. (spot long-only cannot harvest short-leg momentum).
- **Risks:** $1M market-cap floor admits microcaps not on Coinbase; delisting returns not discussed; on-chain features need paid IntoTheBlock data; design-selection risk shown by the robustness grid.
- **Data, cost, licence:** daily OHLCV and market cap including dead coins (free tiers of CryptoCompare/CoinGecko roughly sufficient for price-based features; licences not checked).
- **Repo relation:** overlaps T04 leads already in SEEN.csv (Crypto factor zoo, Lasso anomalies, CTREND) and the survey's verdict that cross-sectional factors mostly fail this account's constraints (line 25). New here: ML-combination results and explicit breakeven costs. No repo hit for Cakici.

### 3. Gençay, "What survives honest evaluation? Leakage-safe, search-aware assessment of LLM-driven trading strategy discovery"
- **Primary URL:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27734
- **Date and type:** 2026-08-27, preprint, single author. Full text read via fetch summary.
- **Summary:** An LLM searches for strategies but can only compose features from a registry with look-ahead structurally removed; every trial is logged and results are deflated by the full trial count (Deflated Sharpe, probability of backtest overfitting, bootstrap intervals), with realistic costs. A deliberately leaky "oracle" strategy is included as a control and passes the deflation tests, showing that multiple-testing corrections do not detect leakage.
- **Claimed effect:** leaky oracle Sharpe 34.7, DSR 1.00; best gpt-4.1 strategy from 102 trials in-sample Sharpe 1.69, held-out 0.18, DSR 0.86 (below 0.95); repeated and alternative-model searches produced no passing strategy; confirming a Sharpe ~0.6 edge needs ~10 years OOS.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** US equities/ETFs only (BTC appears only via one ETF); survivorship in the equity universe; single author, not peer-reviewed.
- **Risks:** method paper; transfer to crypto is by analogy.
- **Data, cost, licence:** reading only; free.
- **Repo relation:** directly relevant to `dsr_btc_eth_trend_20261011/` (DSR alone) and `integrity_audit_20261015/` (look-ahead perturbation). The leaky-oracle control is a check the repo does not appear to have (no grep hit for "oracle" control or PBO in validation files; PBO is mentioned only in the survey).

### 5. LLM look-ahead and memorization critiques (newer than Glasserman & Lin, already in SEEN.csv)
- **Primary URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14765 (Lopez-Lira, Tang, Zhu, "The Memorization Problem"). Supporting: https://arxiv.org/html/2512.23847v2 (Gao, Jiang, Yan, Lookahead Propensity test), https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.14233 (Kong, Lopez-Lira, Zohren et al., checklist), https://arxiv.org/html/2601.13770v1 (Look-Ahead-Bench), https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.24564 (FinCAD).
- **Date and type:** 2025-2026 preprints (Lopez-Lira also SSRN 5217505). Full text via fetch summary except FinCAD (abstract).
- **Summary:** GPT-4o recalls pre-cutoff market values almost exactly and instructions to "ignore" later data do not change accuracy; masked earnings calls are still identified. Gao et al. give a cheap test: ask a date-only question and read first-token probabilities; a positive interaction between this "lookahead propensity" and forecast accuracy flags contamination, which disappears after the cutoff. Kong et al. find only 26.8% of 164 LLM-finance papers acknowledge look-ahead and 1.2% survivorship. Look-Ahead-Bench reports 15-22 pp alpha decay in/out of sample for standard LLMs.
- **Claimed effect:** recall accuracy 97.6% vs 98.0% with/without "ignore" instruction; 1 SD of LAP raises signal effect by 32%; FinCAD memorization correction cuts in-sample returns by up to 67%.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** all equity/macro; none tests crypto. Look-Ahead-Bench author appears linked to a point-in-time model vendor (conflict of interest).
- **Risks:** implies any pre-cutoff LLM-based crypto backtest (sentiment, news, agent) is unreliable.
- **Data, cost, licence:** reading only.
- **Repo relation:** relevant to the news copilot (`edge_map_20261013.md` line 61) and the T06 sentiment leads; no repo hit for memorization or Lopez-Lira.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** add one rule to the research doctrine draft: LLM-derived signals may only be evaluated on data after the model's documented training cutoff; no market data.
- **Status:** UNTESTED
