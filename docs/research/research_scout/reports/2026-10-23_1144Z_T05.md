### 4. Limit vs marketable orders for small retail orders: Anand, Samadi, Sokobin, Venkataraman "Retail Limit Orders", with crypto adverse-selection counter-evidence
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=4964266 (journal page https://revfin.org/retail-limit-orders)
- **Date / type:** Review of Finance 30(2), 2025-26; peer-reviewed (US equities). Reading level: abstract-level summary.
- **Summary:** 27M retail equity orders (FINRA OATS, 19 brokers, May 2020). On implementation shortfall, counting the opportunity cost of unfilled orders, limit orders cost about 10 bp less than marketable orders, more so in high-spread, high-volatility and small-cap names. Fill rates about 60-65%; most orders rested 10+ minutes.
- **Claimed effect:** ~10 bp saving per order, before Coinbase's 25 bp maker/taker fee gap.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** crypto maker fills cluster before adverse moves. DaruFinance/crypto-adverse-selection (https://github.com/DaruFinance/crypto-adverse-selection, MIT, ~125.8M simulated fills on Bybit/Binance/Hyperliquid perps 2023-04 to 2026-05) claims net 10-s markout before fees of -0.39 to -0.84 bp and adverse selection about -0.85 to -1.04 bp per fill; SAIndustries/LOB-Market-Making claims about -1.1 bp at 10 s on BTCUSDT and DOGEUSDT (one test day, 2026-10-01). Albers et al. arXiv 2502.18625 (already cited in survey) claims fill probability and post-fill return are negatively related. All are HFT-horizon, perps or Binance, survivor panels.
- **Risks:** equities with PFOF/NBBO are not crypto; one month of 2020 data. Adverse selection of ~1 bp is small next to a 25 bp fee gap, but opportunity cost of no-fill on a trending Monday is not measured in crypto.
- **Data / cost / licence:** replicable only by logging own Coinbase limit orders (free, but needs live orders and approval).
- **Repo relation:** `maker_taker_prereg_20261011/PREREGISTRATION_draft_v3.md` (BTC/ETH, not frozen) is the natural home; it could add an altcoin arm and an implementation-shortfall metric that charges unfilled orders.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** amend the maker/taker draft to define implementation shortfall including no-fill opportunity cost and to state whether altcoin basket legs are in scope. Status: **UNTESTED**.

### 5. Timing of the rebalance: "The Quarter-Hour Effect" (Kim & Hansen) and "The crypto world trades at tea time" (Brauneis, Mestel, Theissen)
- **Primary URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09426 (also https://ideas.repec.org/a/kap/rqfnac/v64y2025i1d10.1007_s11156-024-01304-1.html)
- **Date / type:** arXiv v2 July 2026 (preprint); RQFA 64(1) 2025 (peer-reviewed). Reading level: Kim & Hansen full text to Section 3; Brauneis et al. abstract only.
- **Summary:** Kim & Hansen, trade-level Binance USDT perps (BTC, ETH, XRP, SOL, DOGE, ADA), Jan 2021-Oct 2024: in the first 10 s of each quarter-hour trades are ~26% more frequent, dollar volume ~32% higher and absolute returns ~26% larger; spikes also at 1- and 5-minute marks and the top of the hour, attributed to scheduled algorithms; they suggest randomising execution around scheduled times. Brauneis et al., 1,940 pairs on 38 exchanges: activity, volatility and illiquidity peak at 16:00-17:00 UTC.
- **Claimed effect:** higher short-horizon volatility at exact round times; implied (not measured) cost penalty for firing orders exactly at 00:00 UTC.
- **Counter-evidence:** neither paper measures spreads or impact for small orders; Angerer et al. (JRFM 2025) find intraday order-book differences statistically significant but economically small. Perps, large coins, sample ends 2024.
- **Risks:** many specifications in the predictability part (overfitting); transfer to thin Coinbase spot altcoins unknown.
- **Data / cost / licence:** free (Binance public trades; Coinbase trades/L2 via own collection).
- **Repo relation:** `daily_model_timing_contract_20261007.md` line 10 models execution at 00:00 UTC bar open; CTREND-OOS draft trades at the Monday open. Hansen/Kim/Kimbrough 2109.12142 already cited in `monday_market_diagnostic_20261012.md`.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** add to the lead-2 preview log a second sample at a randomised offset (e.g. 00:07-00:23 UTC) and at a US-hours time, pre-registered as descriptive only. Status: **UNTESTED**.
