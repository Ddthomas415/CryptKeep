### 3. Perez Riaza & Gnabo, "From depegs to jumps: The role of stablecoin instabilities in crypto market dynamics" (JIMF 2025), with Boido & Jones (2026) as counter-evidence
- **Primary URL:** https://pure.unamur.be/ws/files/112887849/Perez_Riaza_and_Gnabo_2025_.pdf (authors' institutional repository). Counter: https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202605.0497
- **Date / type:** 2025; peer-reviewed. Boido & Jones 2026, peer-reviewed per the page (FinTech 5(3):68).
- **Summary:** Using 5-minute CoinPaprika data for 70 non-stable assets plus USDT (Jan 2022 to Jun 2023), the authors find that the 5 minutes after a USDT depeg (0.5% threshold, 237 events) carry a far higher chance of BTC jumps and market-wide co-jumps, especially for downward depegs, with raised odds for about 4 hours. Their revised method drops an earlier "depegs predict jumps beforehand" finding. Boido & Jones, at daily frequency on four large caps and three stablecoins (2020 to Feb 2026), find weak depeg-to-return causality and stronger causality from returns to depegs.
- **Claimed effect / data:** BTC jump probability 26.2% vs 0.73% control (~36x); co-jumps 18.6% vs 0.48%; with SPY/GOVT jump controls ~5-6x. Boido & Jones: DAI to BTC p=0.043, to BNB p=0.016; stress x depeg on volatility significant in 2 of 12 specifications.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** Boido & Jones; common-cause (macro news) shrinks the effect about 7x in the authors' own controls; the 2026-10-29 week (lead 1) saw large redemptions with no depeg, so depeg events are rare.
- **Risks:** 2022 stress-heavy sample; jump detection choices; not a trading backtest; last ~10k characters of the PDF unread.
- **Data, cost, licence:** 5-minute stablecoin and altcoin prices (free from Coinbase candles for USDT-USD/USDC-USD where listed). pharos.watch depeg archive (MIT on GitHub; API terms not read) as a possible free event list.
- **Relation to repo:** T11-6 (USD vs USDC cash) still open; "depeg" only 3 repo files.
