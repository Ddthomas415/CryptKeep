### 1. Binance.US free historical market data (klines, trades, aggTrades since 2019-09, SHA-256 checksums)
- **Primary URL:** https://www.binance.us/institutions/market-history (announcement: https://support.binance.us/en/articles/9843310-introducing-historical-market-data-from-binance-us-download-for-free)
- **Date and type:** the support article shows 2026-06-03 (with an "updated 11/6/23" note); docs. The main scout read both pages through the fetch tool.
- **Summary:** Binance.US publishes free daily and monthly zip files of candlesticks, trades and aggregated trades for "all trading pairs" back to September 2019. Each zip has a matching checksum file, verified with `sha256sum -c`. It is the only free bulk archive found from a US-regulated spot venue that comes with checksums. Many of its altcoin pairs are also listed on Coinbase.
- **Claimed coverage:** free; September 2019 onward; daily and monthly files. The full list of intervals is not given (the example path uses 12h).
- **Strongest counter-evidence or caveat:**
  - The page does not say whether delisted pairs are kept.
  - The terms forbid, without written consent, trading services that use Binance.US quotes, data feeds built on the data, and paid or ad-supported apps. The page also says the data is informational only, not for investment decisions.
  - Binance.US volume fell sharply after 2023, so recent candles for thin altcoins may be sparse or stale.
  - The IIT MYSTIC lab (https://mystic.cs.iit.edu/datasets/index.html) claims a cleaned Binance.US set of 153 pairs, but states no licence.
- **Risks:**
  - **Survivorship:** unknown.
  - **Stale-price risk:** on illiquid pairs.
  - **Scope:** a US venue that is not Coinbase, so its prices are not Coinbase fills.
  - **Licence:** ambiguity for any use that feeds trading decisions.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free; Binance.US Terms of Use with the restrictions above.
- **Repo relation:** nothing in the repo uses it. It would be a second US-venue source alongside the 12-month Coinbase hourly archive, and a cross-check for `bn_all/` (Binance global).
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** a desk-only provenance card covering the terms text, whether delisted pairs appear in the listing, and how many of the 395 Coinbase USD products have a Binance.US pair. Directory listing only, no download.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

The spot folder holds roughly 1,200 to 1,400 symbol folders (the fetch tool's estimate). Per the third-party README, files are daily trade prints (id, time, price, size, side) from about 2020-12-18, from which 1-minute to 1-hour bars can be built.

  - **Provenance:** cannot be verified without checksums.

  - **Licence:** unknown. The governing terms would be Bybit's general Terms of Service, which were not read.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free; licence UNVERIFIED.
- **Repo relation:** none. A survivorship-light, trade-level complement to `bn_all/`.

### 5. Ticker-migration splices and delisting-return rules in point-in-time panels (a-holm/the-rule-and-compass #145; Aroesler1/Crypto-Stat-Arb)

- **Summary:**

- **Claimed effect:** none on returns. These are data-handling failure modes with concrete numbers.
- **Strongest counter-evidence or caveat:** single-author hobby repos; a-holm says several acceptance items were still unmet at closure; Aroesler1's -99% is an arbitrary setting. Neither is peer-reviewed.
- **Risks:** this is a checklist, not data. Copying the rules without checking them would import someone else's assumptions.

### 10. Insider pre-positioning as a contamination of listing-event data (Félez-Viñas, Johnson, Putniņš) with The Tie 2026 multi-venue listing study
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4184367 (The Tie: https://www.thetie.io/insights/what-does-an-exchange-listing-actually-deliver-in-2026)
- **Date and type:** SSRN preprint, revised 2025-02-03 (abstract and press summary read); The Tie vendor blog, 2026-07-13 (full page).
- **Summary:**
  - **Félez-Viñas et al.:** tag wallets that repeatedly buy before listing announcements. The 2022 version covered 170 Coinbase announcements (2018-09 to 2022-05) and estimated insider trading before 10-25% of them. The revision claims 28-48% across exchanges and at least $30M in profits.
  - **The Tie:** studies 1,844 first-USD-pair listings (2023-01 to 2026-06) on seven venues using CCData daily VWAP net of BTC. It includes delisted tokens and finds broadly similar curves for top-tier and second-tier venues. It claims Coinbase roadmap-to-trading lead times of about 48-72h.
- **Claimed effect:** prices before an announcement may already contain leaked information. Listing datasets therefore need announcement timestamps, not trading-start dates.
- **Strongest counter-evidence or caveat:**
  - The paper is not published, and the revised sample is unseen.
  - The Tie releases no data, uses a single paid vendor, applies no wash-trade filter, and gives no Coinbase-specific numbers. Its text says both 5 and 7 exchanges.
- **Risks:** an announcement-time error of hours turns into look-ahead in any event study.
- **Data, cost, licence:** paper free to read; The Tie data not available.
- **Repo relation:** T02 rows already cover listing studies (Asalio123, Li et al.). ENTR-CB-1 is STOP. This is a provenance caveat for any forward listing log, not a re-test.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** add "announcement timestamp source" as a required field in any future listing-log spec (documentation only).
- **Status:** UNTESTED
