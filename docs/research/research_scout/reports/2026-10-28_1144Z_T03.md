- **Summary:** waelsabri11 forecasts BTC and ETH daily realised vol from Binance 1-minute bars (OOS Jan 2021 to Sep 2026, 2,012 days) with naive, HAR variants, GARCH, GJR-GARCH, GBM and LSTM, then sizes long-only spot with w = min(1, 40%/forecast vol) at 10 bps one-way - the same form as the candidate's scaler. ### 3. Adaptive conformal VaR across about 4,000 crypto assets, robust where GARCH/HAR fail on short histories (Fantazzini, MPRA 121214)

- **Date:** 2024-06-22 (says forthcoming in JRFM; journal version not verified). **Source type:** preprint. Reading level: full text via fetch tool; market-cap group tables not captured.
- **Summary:** Wraps a simple AR(1) forecast in four online conformal methods (FACI, SF-OGD, AgACI, SAOCP) to produce one-day VaR/ES for about 4,000 CoinMarketCap assets, daily Jul 2010 to Jan 2024, and compares with GARCH and HAR on daily range. The online methods need no model fitting and handle short histories, which is the new-entrant case.

- **Data, cost, licence:** free daily data; methods cheap.
- **Relation to repo:** no hits for conformal/Fantazzini; addresses the gap between VOLFC-1 (top-100, 60-day windows) and newly listed coins with little history.
