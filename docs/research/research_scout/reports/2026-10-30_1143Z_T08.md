### 1. Coinbase candles endpoint returns both start and end bars: chunked pulls can silently drop the first bar (GPT-Trader PR #1342), plus REST minute gaps patched from WebSocket trades (qsyn PR #74)
- **Primary URL:** https://github.com/Solders-Girdles-Org/GPT-Trader/pull/1342 (also https://github.com/indigiti/qsyn/pull/74)
- **Date / type:** merged 2026-10-17 and 2026-10-11; forum-grade (GitHub PRs). Full PR pages read.
- **Summary:** A trading project chunked Coinbase candle requests into 300-interval windows. The endpoint treats start and end as inclusive, so each window asked for 301 bars and the 300-bar cap dropped one; overlapping chunks hid the loss except at the very first bar of the overall window. Its own gap check caught it (BTC-USD 911 of 912 hourly bars, 2026-09-03T00:00Z missing; 912/912 after the fix). The second PR reports "3 Coinbase historical minute gaps" during live acceptance and fills the live minute from WebSocket match trades (deduplicated by trade id), refusing to bridge when REST is more than 10 minutes stale and reporting served gaps separately from raw REST gaps.
- **Claimed effect / data:** one missing bar per pull window (PR 1); 3 minute gaps (PR 2); BTC-USD only.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** the PR reviewer did not confirm inclusive-end behaviour against the live API (the fix works either way). PR 2 gives no root cause; its gaps may be no-trade minutes, which Coinbase omits by design (SEEN T08 row "Coinbase candle semantics").
- **Risks:** a missing first bar is a silent survivorship/selection artefact at every window start; WebSocket backfill mixes a live-only source into a "historical" series (provenance must be tagged).
- **Data / cost / licence:** free Coinbase public API; repo licences not checked.
- **Repo relation:** the 395-product hourly archive (`raw_archives_20261009/README.md` lines 16-17) is the natural place to check; its producer was not located.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** a read-only count of hourly bars per product in the frozen `cb/` and `cb2/` archives against expected counts, splitting missing bars into "window-start" and "other", with no refetch. Pass/fail: any product whose only gaps sit at chunk boundaries flags the fetcher.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.
