### 1. Do Solana Exchange Flows Matter? A peer-reviewed, cost-aware out-of-sample null for SOL (Egorov, Grigoriev, Zharikov, Grigorieva)
- **Primary URL:** https://www.mdpi.com/2571-9394/8/4/65 (DOI 10.3390/forecast8040065)
- **Date:** 2026-07-30. **Source type:** peer-reviewed (MDPI Forecasting 8(4):65, open access, CC BY). Reading level: article page; main scout re-opened it; numeric tables not seen.
- **Summary:** The authors build SOL exchange inflow/outflow features from Solscan histories of 101 labelled centralised-exchange hot wallets (including Coinbase, Kraken, OKX), over 41M transfers above 1 SOL, Aug 2020 to Nov 2025. They state four hypotheses (standalone value, incremental value, value after costs, value in feature subsets) and compare baseline, technical-analysis and on-chain feature sets across CatBoost, XGBoost, random forest, LSTM, BiLSTM and an elastic-net logit, on four non-overlapping expanding-origin holdouts from Nov 2023 to Nov 2025.
- **Claimed effect / data:** a null. Exchange-flow variables are CLAIMED weak alone, to add no stable average improvement, and no on-chain subset is CLAIMED to have positive average trading performance after costs; technical features did best. Cost level not stated on the page read.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** none found against it; it agrees with Chi, Chu & Hao (repo survey) and the T01 whale-signal nulls. Its own weakness is that hypotheses are stated but not shown as pre-registered.
- **Risks:** the authors say whale-threshold variables use completed-quarter data (look-ahead in those descriptive variables); the 101-wallet label set is assembled after the fact (not point-in-time); internal exchange transfers may be counted; direction classification only.
- **Data, cost, licence:** paper free (CC BY). Replication would need Solscan or a Solana archive (Solscan Pro is paid) plus a wallet list; no code/data link seen.
- **Relation to repo:** first altcoin-specific, venue-including-Coinbase evidence on the topic; strengthens the prior in the survey file. No repo test exists.

- **Date:** posted 2026-04-24, revised 2026-05-20. **Source type:** preprint. Reading level: abstract (main scout re-opened it); 63-page PDF not read.
- **Summary:** Weekly data on 404 coins, 2020-2026. Two supply-side predictors: an FDV premium (fully diluted value relative to circulating market cap) and a 12-week realised dilution rate. Fama-MacBeth-style tests and portfolio sorts; the author attributes the effect to limited attention to disclosed vesting schedules.
