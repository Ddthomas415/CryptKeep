### 1. Coinbase market states, Price Protection Point and product-state flags (Trading Rules + Advanced Trade REST/WebSocket docs)
- **Primary URL:** https://www.coinbase.com/ru/legal/trading_rules (the copy opened; an /en equivalent probably exists, not opened). Docs: https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/rest-api/public/get-public-product.md ; https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/advanced-trade-api/websocket/product-status.md
- **Date:** undated (page copyright 2026); docs current as read 2026-10-21.
- **Source type:** docs (venue legal rules and API reference). Reading level: rules read at medium depth; API pages read fully.
- **Summary:** The trading rules define the market states Full Trading, Limit Only, Post Only, Cancel-Only, Auction and Trading Halt. After maintenance or an incident, books are CLAIMED to reopen as Cancel-Only, then Auction (at least 1 minute), then Limit Only or Full (at least 10 minutes later), and Coinbase may cancel resting orders before reopening. Market and limit orders fill only up to a Price Protection Point measured from the mid; the remainder is cancelled. The rules state that Coinbase does not use automatic preset price-band circuit breakers for crypto. The public product endpoint (no auth) returns `status`, `is_disabled`, `trading_disabled`, `cancel_only`, `limit_only`, `post_only`, `auction_mode`, `new`, `view_only` (the last only with authenticated `get_tradability_status=true`); the WebSocket `status` channel pushes per-product status changes with a sequence number but no cancel_only/limit_only booleans.
- **Claimed effect / data:** venue mechanics, no market data. Implication CLAIMED by the rules: on Coinbase, any crash protection is discretionary; partial fills are expected at the PPP on thin books.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** no effective date on the rules; `status` has no documented enum; the docs' post_only description is self-contradictory (likely a typo). The help-centre "circuit breakers" page covers equities only, not crypto.
- **Risks:** a backtest that assumes full fills at bar prices on thin altcoins during reopen auctions or PPP truncation would overstate fills; mixing research-scanner flag filters with live behaviour would hide a gap if the live path checks fewer flags.
- **Data needed, cost, licence:** none to buy; public endpoint and docs are free.
- **Relation to repo:** research scanners already filter four flags (`scan_coinbase_gainers.py` line 48); `limit_only`, `post_only`, `auction_mode`, PPP and reopen sequencing found nowhere in docs/research.
