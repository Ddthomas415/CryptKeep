### 1. Market-drawdown de-risking beat vol targeting and Kelly in a crypto-majors long/cash trend sleeve (Howden & Andreev 2026)
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7115459
- **Date:** posted 2026-07-30. **Source type:** preprint (SSRN working paper; student co-author). Reading level: abstract page only.
- **Summary:** A long/cash 30-day time-series momentum rule on 7 majors (BTC, ETH, BNB, XRP, ADA, SOL, DOGE) with an overlay that halves gross exposure when the aggregate crypto market is more than 15% below its trailing peak. The authors say they also tried vol targeting and Kelly sizing and these did not help, whereas the drawdown-state overlay did.
- **Claimed effect / data:** walk-forward out-of-sample net Sharpe 1.41 vs 0.70 buy-and-hold at 25 bps; max drawdown -44.6% vs -84.4%; with overlay Sharpe about 1.53 and max drawdown about -28%. All 96 grid configurations had smaller drawdowns than buy-and-hold; said to hold at 50 bps and on a "survivorship-hardened" universe. Sample period not visible on the abstract page.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** the authors themselves call the 30-day optimum fragile and say it has not met their own forward t>3 bar. CryptKeep's E7 funding brake (another crash-state overlay) was redundant with the trend rule.
- **Risks:** 96-configuration search (multiple testing); costs below Coinbase retail (0.5-0.9% per side); not peer reviewed; drawdown-state rules can whipsaw; 15% threshold likely chosen in sample.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free daily prices plus a market proxy (e.g. top-N cap-weighted index). Paper free on SSRN.
- **Relation to repo:** directly bears on the incumbent's 50% vol target (`composite_v1_20261013/PREREGISTRATION.md` line 13) and is consistent in direction with the reported-only "no vol scaling" sensitivity in `composite_v1_20261013/README.md` line 19 (which is not a passed test). Different trigger from E7 (price drawdown, not funding).
- **Bounded next step (needs operator approval):** desk-read the full PDF for period, universe and the vol-targeting ablation; if adequate, draft (not freeze) a pre-registration of "halve when market > 15% below peak" on the frozen candidate, noting that 2024-26 has already been seen, so a forward-only paper track may be the only clean test.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.

### 2. EWMA as the hard benchmark, and a shorter half-life for low-persistence crypto vol (Alexander & Dakos QF 2023; Araneda; Brini & Lenz)
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4078032 (Quantitative Finance 23(1), 2023, doi 10.1080/14697688.2022.2159505)
- **Date:** SSRN 2022-04; journal 2023-01. **Source type:** peer-reviewed. Reading level: abstract. Supporting: Araneda arXiv 2105.14382 (US stocks; preprint); Brini & Lenz arXiv 2404.04962 (2024 preprint; ~90% read).
- **Summary:** Alexander & Dakos backtest EWMA-type models against GARCH for crypto VaR/ES and covariance at hourly and daily frequency and find no evidence GARCH beats EWMA when EWMA allows asymmetry and heavy tails. Araneda finds the weekly-horizon optimal lambda nearer 0.92 than RiskMetrics' 0.94, and that using last period's optimal lambda beats a fixed one. Brini & Lenz (87 of the Binance top-100, 5-minute data, 2020-Oct 2022, panel HAR, in-sample only) report crypto vol is much less persistent than equity vol, with the first lag dominating.
- **Claimed effect:** EWMA approximately as accurate as GARCH for crypto risk (coins per a citing paper: BTC, ETH, XRP, LTC); low persistence implies faster decay may fit better.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** Alexander & Dakos evaluate risk levels, not cross-sectional ranks, on majors only; Brini & Lenz has no out-of-sample test; Araneda is equities.
- **Risks:** survivor universes (Brini & Lenz, current top-100); choosing lambda after seeing VOLFC-1 is a garden-of-forking-paths risk.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free daily candles already in the repo.
- **Relation to repo:** VOLFC-1 observed EWMA 0.94 at 0.5283 vs B1 0.5123 (`chronos_vol_forecast_20261018/RESULT.md`), explicitly "an observation, not a tested claim". VOLFC-1 forbids retesting that window.
- **Bounded next step:** draft a forward-only pre-registration (weeks after 2026-10-11) comparing trailing 60-day std vs EWMA 0.94 vs one pre-declared shorter lambda (e.g. 0.90) on the same rank-correlation metric, with sample size and pass margin fixed in advance.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.

### 3. Vol-managed portfolios mostly fail out of sample except for momentum; downside-vol and partial-blend variants (Cederburg et al. JFE 2020; Wang & Yan JBF 2021; Xu CFR forthcoming)
- **Primary URL:** https://www.lehigh.edu/~xuy219/research/COWY.pdf (also https://www.lehigh.edu/~xuy219/research/Downside.pdf; https://cfr.ivo-welch.org/forthcoming/papers/xu2024improving.pdf)
- **Date:** 2020, 2021, 2024 (forthcoming). **Source type:** peer-reviewed (first two); working paper forthcoming (Xu). Reading level: intermediate (author PDFs).
- **Summary:** Across 103 equity factors (1926-2016), real-time vol-managed versions beat unmanaged on Sharpe in only 45/103 and on certainty-equivalent return in 31/103; momentum is the exception (all 9 momentum strategies improved). Scaling by downside vol wins 70/103, and fixed-weight blends (25-50% managed) win 80/103, but their advantage fades by about 25 bps cost and turns negative at 50 bps. Xu argues a constant vol target (rather than variance-equating) fixes much of the weakness, improving 148/197 factors with costs up to 40 bps.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** Liu, Tang & Zhou (JPM 2019) report market vol timing drawdowns of 68-93% once look-ahead is removed; DeMiguel et al. (JF 2024; Wiley blocked, SSRN 3982504 abstract used) find a conditional multifactor version survives net of costs, but long-short.
- **Risks:** equities, monthly, long-short factors; tested costs far below CryptKeep's 100-180 bps round trip.
- **Data, cost, licence:** none new.
- **Relation to repo:** the incumbent already uses a capped constant target (the form Xu favours). The cost-sensitivity result bears on whether weekly re-scaling is worth its turnover; see E9 (`candidate_band_20261013/README.md`).
- **Bounded next step:** desk-read only; record in the survey as context, and if the operator wants a sizing variant, pre-declare at most one (e.g. 50/50 blend of unscaled and scaled weight) for a forward paper track.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.

### 5. Optimal rebalancing boundary for target-volatility strategies (Bai, Pachamanova, Steblovskaya, Wallbaum, FMPM 2026)
- **Primary URL:** https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11408-025-00486-5.pdf
- **Date:** online 2025-07-29; Financial Markets and Portfolio Management 40 (2026). **Source type:** peer-reviewed. Reading level: intermediate.
- **Summary:** Adds a no-trade boundary around the target-vol exposure ratio and chooses it to maximise the Omega ratio subject to average vol deviation no worse than the plain strategy. On S&P 500 with block bootstrap, chosen boundaries were 0.5-2.0% and returns 7.13% vs 6.69% with lower costs; optimal boundaries tended to be narrower in high vol (not consistently).

- **Status:** UNTESTED.

### 7. Intraday realized range beats RV; daily Parkinson/Garman-Klass weak, on 30 Binance coins (Lin et al., Mathematics 2026)

- **Date:** 2026-05-18. **Source type:** peer-reviewed (MDPI). Reading level: full text except final ~12k characters.
- **Summary:** 30 Binance coins, 15-minute data, Sep 2019-Dec 2025, log-HAR with expanding window, 1/7/30-day horizons, QLIKE and log-MSE, DMW tests. Realized range as target/predictor beats RV under log-MSE (about 11.7%, 5.2%, 3.7% at 1/7/30 days; wins 63-84% of months); under QLIKE an RV-plus-calendar-boundary proxy is marginally best. - **Relation to repo:** cautions against assuming daily-OHLC range estimators are a free upgrade over trailing std in Module A.
- **Bounded next step:** none beyond noting; only relevant if the operator wants an intraday data pipeline.
