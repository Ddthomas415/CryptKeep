### 1. A Trend Factor for the Cross Section of Cryptocurrency Returns (CTREND)
- **Primary URL:** https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-financial-and-quantitative-analysis/article/trend-factor-for-the-cross-section-of-cryptocurrency-returns/4C1509ACBA33D5DCAF0AC24379148178. Working PDF: https://jfqa.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/24955-trend-factor-cross-section-crypto-returns.pdf
- **Date and type:** JFQA 60(7), Nov 2025 (online Jul 2025). Peer-reviewed.
- **Authors:** Fieberg, Liedtke, Poddig, Walker, Zaremba.
- **Summary:** The authors combine many price and volume technical signals, over several horizons, into one trend score using a cross-sectional ML combination. Coins are sorted weekly into value-weighted quintiles. The paper is unusual in reporting a top-100-only subsample and explicit break-even costs.
- **Claimed effect:**
  - Long-short spread of 3.87%/week (t=5.19). After the Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu 3-factor adjustment, alpha is 2.62%/week.
  - Using only top-100 coins, net returns are 1.90-2.45%/week, at assumed costs of 30 bp long and 40 bp short.
  - Weekly turnover is about 68%. Break-even cost is about 1.41%; significance is lost at about 0.88%.
  - Data: CoinMarketCap, Apr 2015 to May 2022, 3,244 coins above $1M market cap.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:**
  - The sample ends in May 2022, so there is no 2022-26 out-of-sample period.
  - Plain momentum on top coins was negative or insignificant after 2020 (Äijö 2025, in the survey; Grobys et al. 2026, lead 4).
  - The repo's own E6 weekly momentum failed at Coinbase costs.
  - A 68%/week turnover at 0.75-1.0% per round trip costs roughly 25-35%/yr. That is a scout estimate, not a test.
  - No long-leg-only result was found.
- **Risks:**
  - Data-snooping in the ML signal combination.
  - Delisting handling is not described in what was read.
  - Return truncation at 0.5%/99.5%.
  - CMC volume can be fake or inflated.
- **Data, cost and licence:** daily OHLCV and market cap including dead coins. The free CoinGecko route is lead 7. The signals use only price and volume, so Coinbase candles cover the tradable subset.
- **Repo relation:** this is a different signal from E6's single-lookback weekly momentum, but the same family. E6 trial counts must carry forward into any DSR.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** read the full paper and extract (a) long-leg-only and top-100 net figures and (b) the exact signal list. Then decide whether to draft a pre-registration for a *forward-only* paper track: weekly, Coinbase-tradable top-100, long top quintile only, with trial count inherited from E6. No backtest on historical data without approval.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 2. Cryptocurrency as an Investable Asset Class: Coming of Age
- **Primary URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.14435
- **Date and type:** Oct 2025 (v2). Preprint survey with new estimates.
- **Authors:** Borri, Liu, Tsyvinski, Wu.
- **Summary:** The original crypto-factor authors re-estimate their factors on a survivorship-free CoinGecko panel that includes delisted coins. They report a separate post-2020 sample, which is the most direct published update of the Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu factors through 2025.
- **Claimed effect:**
  - Data: 16,468 coins, Dec 2013 to Sep 2025, coins under $1M market cap excluded.
  - 2-week momentum: 2.6%/week (t=3.89) full sample; 2.1%/week (t=3.70) after 2020.
  - Size: weakens to -0.9%/week (t=-1.69) after 2020.
  - Value (price relative to new addresses): -2.3%/week after 2020.
  - 12- and 24-week momentum, volatility, volume and beta are not significant after 2020.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:**
  - No transaction costs and no long-only results.
  - The results are weighted toward the whole universe, not the top 100.
  - The original authors are updating their own factors.
  - Grobys et al. 2026 and Arefev 2026 (leads 4 and 9) find no momentum among survivors or net of costs.
- **Risks:** a 2-week horizon implies high turnover. Sort-direction sign conventions need care.
- **Data, cost and licence:** CoinGecko history (free tier via lead 7). The value factor needs new-address counts (on-chain; vendor cost unknown).
- **Repo relation:** confirms the survey's caveat that momentum is fragile after 2020. Not yet cited in `docs/research/`; grep found no hit for 2510.14435.
- **Next step (needs approval):** read the post-2020 tables. Record whether a top-100 or value-weighted long-leg number exists. Note it in the survey as an update, with no test.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 3. Revisiting the low-volatility anomaly in cryptocurrency markets
- **Primary URL:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v97y2026ics1544612326003818.html (doi 10.1016/j.frl.2026.109851)
- **Date and type:** Finance Research Letters 97, 2026. Peer-reviewed letter.
- **Authors:** Pyo & Jang.
- **Summary:** Coins sorted on ex-ante volatility show low-vol coins outperforming after 2017, across formation and holding windows. The premium is claimed to grow in later subperiods. A low-vol tilt is naturally long-only and low-turnover, which fits the fee constraint better than momentum.
- **Claimed effect:** low-vol outperformance survives crypto-market controls, removal of extreme BTC episodes, and restriction to coins listed before 2019.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:**
  - Borri et al. (lead 2) find volatility not significant after 2020.
  - Older literature (Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu) found it insignificant.
  - Only a blog-level source for this existed in the repo survey before.
- **Risks:**
  - The "listed before 2019" robustness check is itself a survivor filter.
  - Short-letter format; costs not mentioned.
  - Low-vol in crypto may simply be a proxy for BTC/ETH and large caps.
- **Data, cost and licence:** daily closes only. Coinbase candles already exist outside the repo at `$HOME/xsdata/candles`, per `xs_momentum_20261012/README.md`.
- **Repo relation:** the survey (line 27) calls low-vol "insignificant in Liu et al." with only blog support. This is the first peer-reviewed support the scout found.
- **Next step (needs approval):** obtain the full text. If the long-leg monthly-rebalance numbers hold, draft a pre-registration where the benchmark is the existing BTC+ETH candidate, not BTC.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 4. On survivor cryptocurrency momentum
- **Primary URL:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v92y2026ics1544612326001339.html (doi 10.1016/j.frl.2026.109602)
- **Date and type:** FRL 92, 2026. Peer-reviewed.
- **Authors:** Grobys, Sandretto, Äijö.
- **Summary:** This is mainly a counter-evidence lead. Momentum built on nine coins that stayed in the top 100 from 2017 to Aug 2024 shows no effect. Plain momentum on the yearly top 30 is profitable only after trimming outliers, and even then depends on the sample period.
- **Claimed effect:** no momentum among survivors, using weekly data.
- **Counter-evidence to this lead:**
  - The survivor sample is tiny and low-power.
  - Proelss/Schweizer/Buchwalter, FRL 73 (2025), claim momentum works in larger caps: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v73y2025ics1544612324015605.html
- **Risks:** low power; the full text was blocked.
- **Data:** none needed.
- **Repo relation:** consistent with the E6 FAIL and the Äijö 2025 survey entry.
- **Next step (needs approval):** add as a citation to the survey's cross-sectional paragraph, alongside lead 1, so the evidence is shown as contested rather than one-sided.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 5. Crypto factor zoo
- **Primary URL:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finana/v113y2026ics1057521926000645.html (doi 10.1016/j.irfa.2026.105137)
- **Date and type:** IRFA 113, 2026. Peer-reviewed.
- **Authors:** Mercik, Zaremba, Demir.
- **Summary:** The authors apply iterative alpha-based factor selection to 36 crypto factors. Two or three factors absorb all significant alphas: turnover volatility, bid-ask spread, and the new-address-to-price ratio. Liquidity dominates, which suggests much of the factor zoo is an illiquidity premium.
- **Claimed effect:** liquidity factors dominate across weighting schemes and subsamples.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** the paper does pricing tests, not tradable after-cost returns. An illiquidity premium is the hardest kind to harvest at retail spreads. The repo saw a 120 bps GTC spread on Coinbase (`cap_volume_liquidity_20261012.md`).
- **Risks:** abstract only (full text paywalled or blocked). Weighting schemes unknown.
- **Data:** spread data would need order-book snapshots. The repo has some Coinbase book observations.
- **Repo relation:** supports the survey's verdict that size and liquidity premia die under retail fees. Mainly useful as an **avoidance** input.
- **Next step (needs approval):** obtain the full text. Check whether the long leg of the new-address-to-price ratio is liquid enough for the top 100.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 6. Taming crypto anomalies: A Lasso-type factor model
- **Primary URL:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/riibaf/v83y2026ics0275531926000255.html (doi 10.1016/j.ribaf.2026.103298)
- **Date and type:** Research in International Business and Finance 83, 2026. Peer-reviewed.
- **Authors:** Li & Zhu.
- **Summary:** The authors re-test the 49 Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu anomalies in- versus out-of-sample.
- **Claimed effect:**
  - Out of sample, size disappears and a left-tail-risk effect appears.
  - Double-selection Lasso picks market, 2-week momentum and *residual* momentum.
  - The model slightly beats the Liu-Tsyvinski-Wu and IPCA models.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** pricing-model comparisons are not long-only tradable results. 2-week horizons are expensive at Coinbase costs. E6 failed.
- **Risks:** Lasso selection over 49 candidates is a multiple-testing surface.
- **Data:** daily prices and market cap.
- **Repo relation:** residual (BTC-beta-adjusted) momentum is a variant not tried in E6. Any test would add to E6's trial count.
- **Next step (needs approval):** note it only. Consider it only if lead 1's full-text review is positive.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 7. crypto2 R package: survivorship-corrected CoinGecko and CMC universe
- **Primary URL:** https://sstoeckl.r-universe.dev/crypto2/doc/coingecko-integration.html
- **Date and type:** current version (date not shown). Code and data docs.
- **Author:** S. Stöckl (also a co-author of Ammann et al. 2022).
- **Summary:** An R package that pulls CoinGecko and CMC coin lists and daily history. `cg_list(only_active = FALSE)` includes delisted coins, and `cg_history()` claims full-lifetime daily close, volume and market cap on CoinGecko's free tier. Ranking by market cap each day would rebuild a point-in-time top 100 and its new entrants from a source independent of the CMC archive used by ENTR-CB-1.
- **Claimed coverage:** full-lifetime daily market cap for active and delisted coins. CoinGecko's and CMC's delisted sets only partly overlap.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** none on validity was found.
- **Caveats:**
  - Historical listing snapshots are not on the free tier.
  - The no-key recipe relies on CoinGecko website data, so the terms of service need checking.
  - CryptoRank (Aug 2026, methodology not public) claims 71.9% of 1,539 coins that were ever in the top 100 are now dead (https://cryptorank.io/insights/analytics/the-2021-top-100-crypto-five-years-later). That gives a sanity bar for the dead-coin count in any rebuilt universe.
- **Risks:** CoinGecko market-cap and supply revisions could introduce look-ahead.
- **Licence:** not shown on the page.
- **Repo relation:** ENTR-CB-1 used CMC weekly reference prices; this would be a different-source panel. The ENTR-CB-1 prereg bars re-running that historical test on the same CMC archive. Whether a different archive is allowed is an **operator decision** and is not assumed here.
- **Paid alternative:** Concretum's CMC-ID loop (https://concretumgroup.com/building-a-survivorship-bias-free-crypto-dataset-with-coinmarketcap-api/) needs a paid CMC plan and claims about 23k coins.
- **Next step (needs approval):** a read-only licence and terms-of-service review of CoinGecko for research use. No download.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 9. Cross-Sectional Momentum in Cryptocurrency: A Net-of-Costs Replication on Tradable Binance Perpetuals (2020-2026)
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7404139
- **Date and type:** Sep 2026. Unreviewed preprint (8 pages, single independent author).
- **Author:** Arefev.
- **Summary:** On 832 Binance perpetuals, including archived contracts, at about 0.40%/week costs, 1- and 2-week momentum net spreads are not different from zero. The gross 1-week spread is about 0.57%/week.
- **Corroborating weak evidence:**
  - Practitioner repo `prams2104/crypto-momentum-backtest` claims net Sharpe of 0.42 in-sample, -0.48 validation and -2.18 out-of-sample (2025 to Feb 2026) on 25 Binance coins at 20 bps. Breakeven is about 35 bps.
  - Artemis research (https://research.artemis.ai/p/artemis-crypto-factor-model-analysis-820, Jun 2026) claims its momentum factor was -12.2% YTD through May 2026, gross.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** CTREND (lead 1) claims top-100 net profitability through 2022. Unreviewed, low-power sources.
- **Risks:** perpetuals universe, not spot; funding effects; single author.
- **Data:** none needed.
- **Repo relation:** independent of and consistent with the E6 FAIL.
- **Next step (needs approval):** cite it in the survey as weak external corroboration only.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 10. Crypto Factor Investing (long-only design: BTC core + large-cap momentum + small-cap "intangible value")
- **Primary URL:** https://www.sparklinecapital.com/post/crypto-factor-investing
- **Date and type:** Feb 2024, updated May 2025. Fund manager blog.
- **Author:** Kai Wu, Sparkline Capital.
- **Summary:**
  - Universe: coins above $25M market cap, Jul 2017 to Feb 2024.
  - Momentum works best in large caps, with muted recent returns. The small-cap premium is not significant.
  - "Intangible value" from on-chain and developer activity works best in small caps.
  - Proposed long-only mix: 60% BTC, 20% large-cap momentum, 20% small-cap intangible value. This is one of the few explicitly long-only factor designs found, and it points at the mid/low-cap tilt in the standing focus.
- **Claimed effect:** qualitative and chart-based; no cost model.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:**
  - Excludes transaction costs; daily rebalancing; the manager's own backtest.
  - Cakici et al. (IRFA 94, 2024, https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finana/v94y2024ics1057521924001765.html) find long-side ML alphas but concentrated in small, illiquid coins.
- **Risks:** developer-activity data has look-ahead and revision risk, and repo matching is ambiguous.
- **Data:** GitHub developer activity (free API) plus on-chain activity (vendor cost unknown).
- **Repo relation:** overlaps topic T06 (GitHub developer activity). The survey notes Cong et al. value as unverifiable.
- **Next step (needs approval):** none now. Revisit when T06 runs.
- **Status:** UNTESTED
