### 1. The Price of Processing: information frictions after DeFi hacks (Azar, Olivas, Sinha, NY Fed Staff Report 1153)
- **Primary URL:** https://www.newyorkfed.org/medialibrary/media/research/staff_reports/sr1153.pdf
- **Date / type:** April 2025, labelled preliminary; preprint (central-bank working paper).
- **Summary:** The authors separate the moment a DeFi exploit is visible on-chain from the moment it becomes common knowledge via a social-media announcement, and track the protocol's governance token at minute frequency. A Kyle-style model with informed traders explains why prices adjust gradually rather than at once.
- **Claimed effect / data:** 49 hacks, about Sep 2020 to Mar 2024, CryptoCompare minute prices. Governance-token CAR about -17% within 3h of the on-chain event, about -19% at 6h, about -26% to -27% at 24h; about 36% of the 24h drop occurs before the announcement; weak evidence of about -1.55% before the hack. Point estimates; several horizons are significant only at 10% or not at all.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** Nguyen & Putnins (lead 4) call exchange-hack price impacts "transient" (different asset class). Kitzler et al. and Immunefi (SEEN T11) also report drift, so the direction is not contradicted for protocol tokens, but n is small everywhere.
- **Risks:** selection of large, known hacks (survivorship); announcement timestamp is a judgment call; window choice may be fitted; abnormal-return benchmark loosely specified; sample period stated inconsistently. Downward drift is no long-side edge; for a long-only spot bot the use is defensive (exit or avoid).
- **Data, cost, licence:** hack timestamps from lead 2 sources (free); minute bars from Coinbase public candles (free). Real-time on-chain exploit alerts (Forta/Hypernative-type) may cost money.
- **Relation to repo:** no hit for the paper or any hack event study; SEEN T11 Immunefi/Kitzler row is the nearest.
- **Bounded next step (operator approval):** pre-register a defensive rule test: for Coinbase-listed protocol tokens in lead-2 hack lists, measure 1h/6h/24h returns vs BTC from first on-chain timestamp; pass only if the median avoided loss exceeds round-trip cost with a pre-set minimum event count. No run without approval.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.
