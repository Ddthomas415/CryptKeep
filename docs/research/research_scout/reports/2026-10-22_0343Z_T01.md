- **Date:** CryptoQuant/Glassnode docs undated; Glassnode blog 2026-03-13; Coin Metrics PIT for BTC 2026-08-20, ETH and ERC-20 stablecoins 2026-10-05.
- **Source type:** docs (vendor) + blog. Reading level: full text of docs pages; Glassnode blog via own site and a secondary write-up.
- **Summary:** CryptoQuant's BTC, ETH and ERC-20 exchange-flow endpoints carry a warning that history changes as new exchange wallets are found; clustering re-runs every Tuesday 00:00 UTC and the endpoint "does not support" PIT. - **Risks:** look-ahead through back-applied entity labels in any published exchange-flow study (this undercuts Hoang & Baur JBF 2022 https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jbfina/v144y2022ics0378426622002023.html and possibly Chi, Chu & Hao, both read at abstract level); revisions are silent and weekly.

### 2. ETF-flow predictability is largely a timestamp artefact: Fabus, Vartiak, Stalmasekova & Kicova (SSRN 7545019)
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7545019
- **Date:** 2026-09-30.
- **Source type:** preprint. Reading level: abstract page only (two scouts).
- **Summary:** Re-tests spot BTC ETF flows against BTC returns with returns aligned to when flows are knowable. Pairing same-day flows with UTC daily returns includes the ETF session in the "future" return; aligning to the 24h after the 16:00 ET close shrinks the effect, and a window starting after publication shows no positive link. Returns predict next-day flows under every alignment.
- **Claimed effect / data:** 2024-01-11 to 2025-07-28, 386 sessions, BTC only: 4.89 pp per $1B (adj. R2 0.228) same-day UTC; 1.67 (HAC p=0.007, adj. R2 0.036) post-close; no positive association post-publication; prior 24h returns predict flows p<0.001.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** https://github.com/azusayamaguchi/btc-etf-residual-flow (README only; data to 2026-10-13, Farside + Coinbase hourly, NAV-strike aligned) CLAIMS an expanding-window "surprise flow" coefficient of +0.173% per $100M (t=3.43) but raw-flow R2 0.009 and no costs; the author says it may not be tradable. TradeWize (2026-09-27) CLAIMS next-day hit rate after top-decile inflows fell from 74% to 50% between halves. Mazur & Polyzos (J. Alt. Investments 2025) CLAIM R2 95% on price levels, a spurious-regression risk.
- **Risks:** single asset, short sample, cash-creation regime; Farside figures are estimates and revised.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free daily flows; Farside does not license redistribution.
- **Relation to repo:** directly qualifies the Lim t=3.12 citation in `crypto_trading_edges_survey_20261013.md` line 37.
- **Bounded next step (needs operator approval):** desk-read the full Fabus et al. PDF if accessible and add a correction note to the survey's ETF-flow sentence; no data pull.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.

### 4. Stablecoin issuance and altcoin returns: Ante, Fiedler & Strehle vs. the null literature
- **Primary URL:** https://www.blockchainresearchlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/The_Influence_of_Stablecoin_Issuances_on_Cryptocurrency_Markets_BRL_Working_Paper_No_11.pdf (journal version FRL 41, 2021: https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/finlet/v41y2021ics1544612320316810.html)
- **Date:** 2020 (WP); 2021 (FRL).
- **Source type:** preprint / peer-reviewed. Reading level: WP full text; FRL abstract.
- **Summary:** Event study of 565 issuances of $1M+ across seven stablecoins, hourly Bitstamp prices for BTC, ETH, XRP and LTC. Prices dip in the week before, run up before the event, and drift up for 24h after; issuance size does not matter. One of very few tests that includes altcoins.
- **Claimed effect / data:** Apr 2019-Mar 2020: 0-24h CAR about +0.57% BTC, +0.47 to +0.69% across the four coins; nothing in the first 2h; USDT alone significant only at 10%.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** Wei 2018 Economics Letters (https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/ecolet/v171y2018icp19-22.html: no predictive effect, returns lead Tether); Lyons & Viswanath-Natraj NBER w27136 (no significant BTC/ETH effect); CrossTower 2021 (mints follow price). Saggu FRL 2022 finds a BTC effect only over 5-30 minutes.
- **Risks:** one year, one venue; mean-return benchmark after a dip can manufacture drift; on-chain "mint" vs treasury authorisation (Tether authorises before issuing) muddies event time; claimed drift is at or below 0.5-1.0% round-trip cost.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free (explorers; Circle weekly mint/burn on its transparency page; Tether daily circulation, no download).
- **Relation to repo:** not covered beyond the Chi et al. line in the survey.
- **Bounded next step (needs operator approval):** none recommended on the history (old, cost-marginal); at most a desk note recording the null-vs-positive split for the survey.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.

- **Primary URL:** https://github.com/zty05070242/whale-signals (also https://panews.io/articles/1r6emlot, https://blog.quantinsti.com/does-whale-activity-predict-bitcoin-a-pre-registered-machine-learning-test-epat-project/)

- **Source type:** docs/code (MIT); blog (industry research, translated); blog (practitioner). Reading level: README; full article; full article.
- **Summary:** whale-signals studies 646k ETH transfers over $1M from a free Dune query, labelled with two open label lists (62.8% coverage) plus a classifier, with 2026 as out-of-sample; unconditional hit rate about a coin flip, deposit-as-sell improving with horizon. - **Relation to repo:** none.

### 9. Cross-chain negative spillovers among L1 ecosystems: Ma, Bao & Wen (arXiv 2602.23762)
- **Primary URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.23762
- **Date:** 2026-02.
- **Source type:** preprint. Reading level: abstract only.
- **Summary:** Using on-chain activity for Ethereum, Solana, BSC, Arbitrum and Avalanche (2022-2025), reports that a surge on one chain coincides with declines on others, explained as attention-driven capital reallocation, robust to BTC, equity and rate controls. Conceptually relevant to rotation among altcoin L1 tokens.
- **Claimed effect / data:** negative spillovers that strengthen in attention shocks; no magnitudes or trading test in the abstract.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** none found; the review arXiv 2606.00071 notes on-chain predictors often fail OOS (Yae & Tian 2022; Shanaev et al. 2019 find network-activity/price links spurious under IV).
- **Risks:** survivor chains; flexible attention-shock definitions; contemporaneous, not predictive, as described.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free chain activity (explorers, BigQuery, Artemis/Token Terminal free tiers not verified).
- **Relation to repo:** none; cross-sectional factor work (T04 SEEN rows) did not include chain-activity rotation.
- **Bounded next step (needs operator approval):** desk read of the full PDF to see whether any lagged (predictive) specification exists before considering a hypothesis.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.
