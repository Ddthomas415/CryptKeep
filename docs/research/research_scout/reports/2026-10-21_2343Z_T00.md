### 1. Funding intervals now switch per contract and per event on Binance, OKX and Bybit (data-integrity lead)
- **Primary URL:** https://www.okx.com/en-us/help/perps-funding-fee-mechanism (OKX help 360053909272, updated 2026-08-27). Also Bybit Dynamic Settlement Frequency release (effective 2025-10-30, full rollout 2025-11-03); Binance 2026-01-02 announcement (read via treeofalpha preview, see header); https://github.com/freqtrade/freqtrade/issues/12583 (about Nov 2025).
- **Date:** 2023-09-28 (Binance 4h/±3% caps) to 2026-08-27.
- **Source type:** docs (venue rules) + forum/issue tracker. Reading level: OKX page and freqtrade issue opened; Bybit release opened; Binance 2025-05-02 rule blocked.
- **Summary:** On all three venues an altcoin perp that hits its funding cap is moved to a shorter interval (OKX steps 8h->4h->2h->1h; Bybit straight to 1h; Binance into 1h) and later moved back automatically (Binance: after 16 hourly prints with |rate| <= 0.025%; OKX: after 12h within ±0.20%; Bybit "without prior notice"). Caps themselves can change in real time (OKX) or with leverage cuts (Binance). The freqtrade issue CLAIMS the APIs expose only the current interval, not its history, and that about 60% of Binance USDT-M contracts were on 8h as of 2025-11-29.
- **Claimed effect / data:** mechanics, not returns. Implication: per-print rates from altcoin history are not comparable across rows unless the interval for that print is known; annualising with x3/day overstates or understates by up to 8x; using today's `fundingInfo` interval for past prints is look-ahead.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** none against the mechanics; BTC/ETH are largely excluded (Bybit says so), so E7 (BTC/ETH, Binance) is probably unaffected (not checked).
- **Risks:** look-ahead (current interval applied to history); mis-scaled extremes in any altcoin `funding_extreme` threshold; silent regime changes from cap changes.
- **Data, cost, licence:** free public endpoints; interval derivable from consecutive `fundingTime` gaps.
- **Relation to repo:** collector stores `interval_hours` (`REMAINING_TASKS.md` line 992) but its source (venue-reported current value vs derived from timestamps) was not checked; `funding_carry_feasibility_20261012.md` already flags interval-change certification as unverified.
