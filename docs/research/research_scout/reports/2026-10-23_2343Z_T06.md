### 1. Crypto-influencer tweets: small up-move, then a larger reversal, strongest in smaller caps (Merkley, Pacelli, Piorkowski, Williams), with Telegram pump evidence (Clough & Edwards; Moura et al. 2026)
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4412017 (also https://blog.kelley.iu.edu/2024/11/21/research-be-cautious-in-following-crypto-influencers-investment-advice-most-gains-disappear-within-days/ , https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06608 , https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01176)
- **Date / type:** Merkley et al., "Crypto-Influencers", Review of Accounting Studies 2024 (peer-reviewed; SSRN abstract and university summary read; journal page on Springer not opened). Clough & Edwards, arXiv 2023 (preprint, abstract). Moura, Paoletti, Ferreira, Almeida, arXiv 2026-09-01 (preprint, abstract).
- **Summary:** About 36,000 tweets by 180 prominent crypto influencers on 1,600+ assets (two years to Dec 2022). Mentions go with a short positive move, then negative longer-horizon returns; the reversal is strongest for self-described experts, for influencers with many followers, for smaller-cap assets and for bullish/buy-recommendation tweets. Clough & Edwards, using Telegram pump events on 765 coins, report an average ~30% relative price drop one year after a pump. Moura et al. scan 14,499 public Telegram channels (20M+ messages, one year) and find 47 pump-and-dump-consistent events whose messages precede price moves by seconds and read like ordinary discussion. Together these extend the SEEN Ardia & Bluteau lead with a much larger asset set and a non-organised-pump sample.
- **What is new vs SEEN:** SEEN (2026-10-20, T06 lead 1) has Ardia & Bluteau, Moser & Brauneis, Qureshi & Zaman (pump events, YouTube, ICO cohorts). New: a peer-reviewed sample of organic influencer mentions across 1,600 assets with day-by-day magnitudes, and a 2026 Telegram-scale study.
- **Claimed effect:** mean return +1.83% day 1, +1.57% day 2; days 2-5 -1.02%; cumulative -2.24% at 10 days and -6.53% at 30 days (Kelley summary of Merkley et al.); for "small, lesser-known tokens" a buy-on-tweet 30-day hold loses 7.9% (no baseline stated). Clough & Edwards: ~-30% at one year after pump.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** Maître et al. (SEEN) find no reversal after organic Twitter attention; Haase et al. (SEEN) find crowd buy signals predict positive short-term moves in small coins. Söylemezgil (Bilkent MBA thesis 2023, top-10 coins, Twitter+Reddit+Google Trends PCA index) finds attention does not explain returns, while returns drive attention. All samples end by 2022-2025; magnitudes are not cost-adjusted and benchmark adjustment is not stated in the summary.
- **Risks:** influencer list chosen ex post (survivorship of "prominent" accounts); returns measured from tweet time, which a daily-bar strategy cannot capture; the X data used is not reproducible at retail cost (lead 7). Pump participation is illegal and excluded (survey line 31); only avoidance is in scope.
- **Data / cost / licence:** historical X data is not affordable (full-archive search requires Pro, about $5,000/month, CLAIMED). A forward Telegram log of public channels is technically free but unscoped.
- **Repo relation:** consistent with the survey's "attention front-loaded" verdict; no repo rule exists for excluding recently hyped coins.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** a one-page pre-registration of an *avoidance* filter only: define a free, timestamped hype flag (e.g. the existing CMC trending log entry, line 60 of `edge_map_20261013.md`) and test whether coins flagged in the prior 5 days underperform unflagged peers over 10-30 days, on forward data accumulated after the freeze. Status: **UNTESTED**.

### 2. Sentiment beta priced in the cross-section, measurable with the free Fear & Greed history (Han JBEF 2025), with CryptoSent (John, Li, Liu) and music-sentiment beta (Hadhri et al. JIMF 2025)
- **Primary URL:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/beexfi/v46y2025ics2214635025000243.html (also https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4941032 and https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jimfin/v157y2025ics0261560625001299.html)
- **Date / type:** Han, "Investor sentiment and cross-section of cryptocurrency returns", Journal of Behavioral and Experimental Finance 46 (2025), peer-reviewed, abstract only (ScienceDirect not opened). John, Li, Liu, SSRN working paper, revised 2026-04-06, abstract only. Hadhri, Younus, Naeem, Yarovaya, JIMF 157 (2025), peer-reviewed, abstract only.
- **Summary:** Han estimates each coin's return sensitivity to the Crypto Fear & Greed Index ("sentiment risk") and sorts coins on it, Nov 2018 to Jul 2024, controlling for market, size, reversal and liquidity factors: intermediate-sensitivity coins out-earn both low- and high-sensitivity coins, and high positive sentiment beta carries a negative premium, attributed to overpaying for lottery-like coins. John-Li-Liu build a CryptoSent index and report that coins with high absolute sensitivity earn lower returns the next week and month; adding a sentiment mispricing factor raises a three-factor model's explanatory power by 13%. Hadhri et al. report the same negative sign for sensitivity to a Spotify mood index on 2,551 coins over five years. Three independent sentiment proxies with the same sign is the interesting part; all three are in-sample.
- **Claimed effect:** Han: intermediate-sentiment-risk coins earn weekly risk-adjusted returns about 3.57% higher (abstract). John-Li-Liu and Hadhri: no magnitudes on the abstract pages.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** 3.57% per week is implausibly large for a tradable strategy and suggests illiquid micro-caps drive it; no cost treatment in any abstract. The survey (table row 7) grades F&G timing "weak"; this is a different use (cross-sectional beta, not timing) but the same index. Wu et al. (JIMF 2025, abstract) find attention-based portfolio tilts work in calm markets and fail in turbulent ones.
- **Risks:** beta estimated on overlapping windows (look-ahead if the estimation window touches the sort date); survivorship in CoinGecko/CMC universes; three-group sort chosen after seeing the hump (overfitting); weekly rebalancing at 0.50%+ per side would likely consume most of any spread at retail cost.
- **Data / cost / licence:** alternative.me F&G daily history is free (already read by `services/market_data/alternative_data.py`; licence/attribution terms not checked this run). Prices from existing repo sources.
- **Repo relation:** no repo work on sentiment betas. A lower-value top-100 universe (`docs/research/low_value_top100_20261018/`) exists as a possible host.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** read Han's full text through an approved route, then, if the effect survives in large/mid caps, draft a pre-registration of a monthly (not weekly) sentiment-beta tilt on the lower-value top-100 universe with a fixed estimation window, cost at 0.50%/0.90% per side and a single pre-declared sort. Status: **UNTESTED**.

### 4. Polymarket token-launch markets were systematically over-optimistic, and over-optimism went with worse post-launch returns (Legion/PANews 29-event study; PolymarketWhale 10-project accuracy study)
- **Primary URL:** https://panews.io/articles/e52297d8-54a7-469c-ada0-96be7b8c151c (also https://paragraph.com/@polymarketwhale/how-effective-is-polymarket-fdv-prediction-market)
- **Date / type:** PANews (compiling Legion's WazzCrypto), 2026-01-31, blog; PolymarketWhale on Paragraph, about 2026-01-19, blog (contains Polymarket referral links: conflict of interest). Both read in full via fetch summary.
- **Summary:** PANews: 231 Polymarket markets on 29 token-sale events (~$241.5M volume). One week before close, 24h-FDV markets were right 62.5% of the time and overestimated FDV by about +35% on average; 75% of tokens sold off in their first 24 hours; over-optimism correlated with ICO returns at r = -0.41 (5 tokens). PolymarketWhale: 10 projects (EigenLayer, Monad, Pump.fun, Meteora, Lighter and others): median-implied FDV error 128% a month before TGE, 44% a week before, 21% three days before, about 12% at D-1, with early overvaluation converging downward; Meteora an outlier (102%).
- **What is new vs SEEN:** the SEEN Polymarket FDV lead proposed the markets as forward data without any accuracy evidence. New: two (small) accuracy studies showing an optimistic bias and a possible negative link to post-launch returns.
- **Claimed effect:** +35% average FDV overestimate; r = -0.41 between over-optimism and ICO returns (n = 5); 24h FDV accuracy 62.5% (n = 8).
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** samples are tiny (5, 8, 10), no significance tests, post-hoc signals; PolymarketWhale finds final-day error of only ~12%, i.e. the market is roughly right by D-1. The figures are inconsistent in places (113 vs 88 points in the second study).
- **Risks:** selection (only tokens with public ICOs or liquid markets); outcome-dependent settlement rules; most launches are not on Coinbase on day 1, so the trade may not be executable for CryptKeep.
- **Data / cost / licence:** free (lead 3 archives plus CoinGecko/Coinbase prices).
- **Repo relation:** ENTR-CB-1 ended STOP (`coinbase_tradable_entrants_20261019/RESULTS_V1.md`); this is forward/prediction-market data, not a re-run on the CMC archive. Fits an avoidance rule for new entrants.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** pre-register an avoidance hypothesis: for new tokens later listed on Coinbase, a high Polymarket-implied FDV relative to day-1 FDV predicts weaker 30-day returns; build the event list from lead 3 metadata only after freeze. Status: **UNTESTED**.

### 6. Kalshi macro-contract probabilities and five-day crypto realised vol, including SOL, ADA, AVAX, LINK (Mohanty & Krishnamachari)
- **Primary URL:** https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01431v1
- **Date / type:** arXiv 2026-04-01, preprint; full text read via fetch summary.
- **Summary:** Daily changes in volume-weighted Kalshi probabilities for eight macro series (Fed cut/dovish, CPI, recession and others), Jan 2023 to Mar 2026, added to a HAR-plus-controls model of five-day-ahead realised vol for BTC, ETH, SOL, ADA, AVAX and LINK. Expanding-window out-of-sample tests with Clark-West, Benjamini-Hochberg across 60 specifications.
- **Claimed effect:** out-of-sample MSFE ratios slightly below 1 for some pairs (BTC recession 0.979, p=0.020; ETH CPI 0.959, p=0.010; SOL CPI 0.983, p=0.048; ADA monetary 0.992, p=0.041); only BTC-Fed-dovish and LINK-CPI survive BH at q=0.05 (in-sample). The strongest BTC in-sample signal has no OOS gain (1.009).
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** the authors report the Fed-dovish gain built in the 2024-25 cutting cycle and reversed after; "best signal by asset" was chosen in-sample; OOS windows are 144-269 observations; no bid-ask data. Gains of 1-4% in MSFE are small next to the turnover finding in SEEN T03 (better vol forecasts mostly cut turnover).
- **Risks:** multiple testing beyond the 60 reported cells; risk-neutral probabilities; short sample.
- **Data / cost / licence:** Kalshi historical endpoints (lead 5) free; CoinGecko prices.
- **Repo relation:** belongs with T03 sizing work (SEEN T03 leads on EWMA/HAR); repo vol work in `chronos_vol_forecast_20261018/`.
