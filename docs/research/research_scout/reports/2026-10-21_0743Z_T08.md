### 2. "Aggregate Confusion in Crypto Market Data" (Schwenkler, Shah, Yang)
- **Primary URL:** https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2025.00611 (online appendix: https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/suppl/10.1287/mnsc.2025.00611/suppl_file/mnsc.2025.00611.sm1.pdf; full PDF copy: https://www.sec.gov/files/cryptodata-sec-102525.pdf; SSRN 4673905)
- **Date and type:** Management Science, 2025/26 (appendix dated 2026-04-20; SEC copy 2025-10); peer-reviewed. Full PDF (SEC copy) read via fetch summary by one sub-scout; appendix read by another.
- **Summary:** An audit of crypto data aggregators. Prices, returns, market caps and especially volumes disagree across providers, much more for small coins; coin identifiers are unstable (ID reuse and undisclosed changes, token swaps/redenominations). Factor and size portfolios built from different providers overlap little. The authors propose a filtered cross-provider median and grade providers.
- **Claimed effect:** sample Nov 2018 to Oct 2024, 8 aggregators in the main paper (20 tracked in appendix). Only ~30% of provider-day volume entries within +/-5% of the median; up to 21% of Coinpaprika coins with undisclosed ID changes, ~16% of CoinGecko sample with reused IDs; CMC 48 coins affected by forks/swaps; provider-based returns overstated by up to 10.8%/yr (CoinGecko size factor). Grades: CMC and Santiment A (not independent), Messari B+, CoinGecko C on average.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** Vidal-Tomás (IRFA 2022, lead 6) finds aggregators agree for the most liquid coins; reconcilable because the problems concentrate in the long tail. The median benchmark is not ground truth.
- **Risks:** survivorship not analysed directly; sample ends 2024; aggregated dataset not found publicly.
- **Data, cost, licence:** paper free to read; no data licence found.
- **Repo relation:** directly bears on D_CMC and CoinGecko snapshots, and on lower-value top-100 work. No repo hit for this paper.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** a desk note mapping the paper's identifier-failure categories onto the repo's identity maps (`coinbase_tradable_entrants_20261019/identity_map_v3`, adjudication JSONs) to see whether those failure types were already handled; reading only.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

- **Date and type:** NBER 2022 / Management Science; critique preprint v. Feb 2025. Abstract-level reading.
- **Summary:** Cong et al. claim wash trading averaged over 70% of reported volume on unregulated exchanges (29 exchanges), while regulated venues looked normal.
