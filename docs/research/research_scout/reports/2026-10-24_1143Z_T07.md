### 1. Wang & Pang, "Predictability on the Move: The Cryptocurrency Cross-Section"
- **Primary URL:** https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7556359
- **Date and type:** dated 2026-10-03, posted 2026-10-06; preprint (Wang at HKUST). Abstract read by a sub-scout and re-read by the main scout; full PDF not read.
- **Summary:** The authors rebuild published crypto factors on a point-in-time Binance universe and run 1,043 signals (up to 750,960 signed candidates) through full-sample data-snooping controls and Romano-Wolf StepM, then repeat on rolling 104-week windows. Conventional tests flag thousands of strategies; the multiple-testing controls select none over the full sample. Rolling windows show predictability switching on and off, and the later-window survivors are mostly one-day liquidity and trading-activity signals.
- **Claimed effect:** full sample: no candidate with positive expected performance at 5%; StepM selects in 1 of 24 early windows vs 24 of 28 later windows; later selections stay positive over the next 78 weeks "at low costs" (cost level not in the abstract). Published size, momentum and trend premia weaken.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** none yet (6 weeks old, no citing papers found). Its own result: the surviving signals are one-day, which a 50 bps round trip at daily/weekly horizons likely cannot carry.
- **Risks:** sample dates and cost model unseen; rolling re-selection is itself a search; Binance universe differs from Coinbase.
- **Data, cost, licence:** reading only; free.
- **Repo relation:** directly relevant to CTREND-OOS (`ctrend_oos_20261022/PREREGISTRATION_DRAFT.md`): it CLAIMS trend premia weaken on a point-in-time universe. Also a template for program-level multiple-testing (StepM) beyond DSR (`dsr_btc_eth_trend_20261011/`). No repo hit for StepM.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** read the full PDF and add a short note to the CTREND-OOS review file: what window, cost and universe the trend result used, and whether the draft's pass/fail should cite it as prior counter-evidence. No market data.
- **Status:** UNTESTED

### 2. Chu, Shen & Zhu, "Machine Forecast Disagreement in the Cryptocurrency Market" (JFQA, accepted)
- **Primary URL:** https://jfqa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/26316_Machine_Forecast_Disagreement.pdf (equity original: Bali et al., NBER w31583, https://www.nber.org/papers/w31583)
- **Date and type:** June 2026 manuscript; peer-reviewed (accepted). Full text read via fetch summary; Internet Appendix (costs) not seen.
- **Summary:** 100 simulated random-forest "investors" each see a random 24 of 40 coin characteristics (depth-3 trees, refit every 12 weeks on 104 weeks). Machine forecast disagreement (MFD) is the cross-sectional dispersion of the 100 forecasts per coin. High-MFD coins underperform next week, most where arbitrage is hard and after high sentiment.
- **Claimed effect:** CMC data, 3,024 coins, main tests 2019-01 to 2024-06: value-weighted high-minus-low about 1.79%/week; equal-weighted 1.22%/week (t=2.60); Fama-MacBeth slope -7.8 (t=-3.5); high-limits-to-arbitrage subset -3.74%/week. Profits said to survive "conservative" costs (appendix not seen).
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** equal-weighted spread dies after one week; concentrated in high-friction coins; smallest coins need winsorising; lead 1 suggests crypto cross-sectional effects are unstable over time.
- **Risks:** CMC prices are not executable; large hyperparameter space; sub-scout flagged a possible sign inconsistency in the text and inconsistent filter thresholds; delisting handling CLAIMED but unverified.
- **Data, cost, licence:** daily OHLCV/market cap incl. dead coins (existing Coinbase candle archive covers price-based features); CPU-cheap.
- **Repo relation:** long-only spot can use only the "avoid high-MFD" side, as a filter on an existing long book (e.g. the CTREND top quintile). No repo hit. Overlaps the T04 factor rows in SEEN.csv only in characteristic set.
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** desk read of the Internet Appendix cost table and the sign question; if it holds, draft (do not freeze) a pre-registration of MFD as an exclusion filter on Coinbase top-100 with a fresh forward window. No market data.
- **Status:** UNTESTED
