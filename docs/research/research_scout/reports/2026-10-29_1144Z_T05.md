### 4. Square-root impact on altcoins: Barone & Lillo (Hyperliquid, 201 perp markets) vs Maitrier, Loeper & Bouchaud (synthetic metaorders from public tapes)
- **URLs:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.15715 (2026-06-14, preprint; PDF read by sub-scout, abstract re-read verbatim) ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18199 (Mar/Apr 2025, preprint; PDF read).
- **Summary:** Barone & Lillo use Hyperliquid's address-level on-chain fills to compare 465k visible TWAPs with 4.3M reconstructed hidden metaorders. Abstract (verbatim-checked): TWAPs trade nearly uniformly and pay lower costs and smaller permanent impact; visible TWAPs draw displayed depth. From the PDF (sub-scout read only, not in abstract): about 201 perp markets mostly altcoins, Jul 2025-Mar 2026, about 641M fills, TWAP impact scaling roughly participation^0.30, Y about 0.19, about 9 bps lower temporary and 5 bps lower permanent impact for TWAPs. Maitrier et al. build synthetic metaorders from anonymous trade tapes and recover impact ~ Y*sigma*sqrt(Q/V) with Y about 0.5 on EuroStoxx futures and Euronext stocks (no crypto).
- **Claimed effect:** a cheap route to an impact prefactor per Coinbase pair from free public trade prints (Maitrier method), with Barone & Lillo as crypto-altcoin counter-evidence that the exponent may be well below 0.5.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** the two disagree (0.3 vs 0.5); Donier & Bonart (arXiv 1412.4503) report square-root on BTC; Talos (lead 10) CLAIMS square-root underestimates small-order cost by about 4 bps. At $1K-$10K orders, impact is likely small next to a 0.50% taker fee (unmeasured).
- **Risks:** metaorder reconstruction is statistical inference; Hyperliquid perps and on-chain design may not transfer to Coinbase spot; no per-coin breakdown.
- **Data / cost:** Coinbase public trades free via API; Hyperliquid fills free.
- **Repo relation:** no hits for Barone, metaorder or impact model in execution docs.
- **Bounded next step (needs approval):** a pre-registration sketch for estimating a per-product impact floor on 5-10 lower-cap Coinbase USD pairs from public trades, with pass/fail = whether impact at the account's order size exceeds 10 bps. Not run.
- **Status:** UNTESTED.

### 5. Cheap daily cost proxies for top-100 coins: Brauneis, Mestel, Riordan & Theissen (JBF 2021) estimator benchmark, with Farzulla's Corwin-Schultz overstatement and wash-volume caveats
- **URLs:** https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jbfina/v124y2021ics0378426620303022.html (JBF 124, 2021; peer-reviewed; abstract only, Mannheim full text behind bot check) ; https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07018 (Feb 2026; preprint; full text).
- **Summary:** Brauneis et al. benchmark low-frequency liquidity measures against order-book liquidity across exchanges and coins: Corwin-Schultz and Abdi-Ranaldo track changes over time best, Kyle-Obizhaeva and Amihud levels and cross-venue differences. Farzulla's side validation CLAIMS Corwin-Schultz correlates about 0.41-0.43 with quoted spreads but its mean (about 141 bps) is far above quoted spreads (about 7 bps Bybit, about 0.36 bps Binance) for BTC/ETH 2024-2026, and is negative on 3% of days.

Farzulla's own tables do not reconcile.
- **Risks:** pre-2020, BTC-heavy sample; level bias.
- **Data / cost:** free daily OHLCV.
- **Repo relation:** related to SEEN EDGE estimator (2026-10-20); no hits for Corwin, Abdi or Amihud.

### 6. Shadow-fill design: shadowfill (Kaplan-Meier fill-probability bias) as template for the repo's would-be-fill recorder
- **URL:** https://github.com/sakshamg251206/shadowfill . **Date:** last commit not shown; 90 commits, 0 stars. **Source type:** GitHub (README read). Licence not stated.
- **Summary:** A pre-registered study in which each real passive order gets a never-cancelling shadow twin whose queue position advances with cancellations and trades; the twin's fill time is the ground truth, compared with a Kaplan-Meier estimate treating cancellations as censoring.
- **Claimed effect:** on AAPL Nasdaq ITCH (2019-12-30), KM gave 16% filled within 60s vs 55% truth; a second session 12% vs 59%; a placebo near zero; 4 of 5 pre-registered hypotheses not supported.
- **Counter-evidence:** equities only; needs L3 order IDs; 1-2 sessions; one author; truth assumes FIFO and no hidden liquidity.
- **Risks:** direction of bias may differ on crypto; small sample.
- **Data / cost:** free; Coinbase L3 exists (SEEN Tardis first-of-month).
- **Repo relation:** `slippage_measurement_review_20261004.md`; REMAINING_TASKS item 15 ("estimate limit-fill probability from subsequent price paths") - this lead suggests naive survival estimates can be badly biased.
- **Bounded next step (needs approval):** add a design note to the shadow-recorder plan stating which fill-probability estimator would be used and why, citing this bias; no code change.
