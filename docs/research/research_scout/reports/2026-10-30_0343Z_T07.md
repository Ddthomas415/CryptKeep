### 1. Sahay, "How much of crypto deep-learning forecasting skill is leakage? A factorial audit with placebo tests" (data + `leakcheck` code)
- **Primary URL:** https://zenodo.org/records/23339885
- **Date and type:** v1.0, 2026-10-13; preprint-style data/code release (CC BY 4.0 record, MIT code), single author, not peer-reviewed. Record page read; archive not downloaded.
- **Summary:** A factorial design switches five common leaks on and off (full-series scaling, random splits of overlapping windows, two-sided features, same-day inputs, test-set selection) on 24 Binance USDT pairs, daily bars, test period 2024-01-01 to 2026-09-19. Placebo series (driftless GBM, shuffled days, block shuffles) check whether a pipeline "finds" skill where none can exist. The leak-free protocol is compared with a random walk using Diebold-Mariano, Clark-West, Model Confidence Set, SPA and reality-check tests. It ships a `leakcheck` module with a six-test reviewer checklist and a coded audit of 64 open-access crypto deep-learning papers.
- **Claimed effect:** share of reported skill attributable to leakage is in `headline_numbers.csv`, not visible on the record page; not known to this scout.
- **Strongest counter-evidence:** none found (2 weeks old). Forecast-accuracy only, no trading costs.
- **Risks:** universe likely current survivors; single author; the 64-paper coding is one reader's judgement.
- **Data, cost, licence:** reading only for the checklist; free; CC BY 4.0 / MIT.
- **Repo relation:** complements the CTREND-OOS planted-leak control and VOLFC-1's protocol; the placebo-series idea (GBM, block-shuffle) is not in the repo (no grep hit for placebo outside `unlock_data_feasibility_20261020.md`).
- **Next step (needs operator approval):** desk read of the paper text and checklist (download of the record is a separate approval); map the six checks against the CTREND-OOS r2 controls and note any missing one in its review file. No market data.
