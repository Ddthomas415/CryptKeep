## Infrastructure and positive gain

The user's approximate shared infrastructure cost is $20/month, or $240/year.
At $1,000 capital that is a 24% annual capital hurdle before any additional costs;
at $10,000 it is 2.4%. These are cost ratios, not required strategy forecasts.
Assumption: the $20 estimate is a shared flat cost. If inaccurate, replace the
scenario with the actual bill; do not charge each hypothetical arm and sum it.

For the recent dynamic window, assigning $40 for the nominal two-month span gives
breakout15 $526.14 marked gain after infrastructure, BTC $252.32, ETH $271.63,
top3 daily seven-day rotation $105.96, and breakout8 -$14.82. These are allocation
scenarios, exclude terminal liquidation, and are not realized net dollars.
The alternative trading-hour allocation is retained in the dynamic scorecard;
do not mix the two conventions or annualize recent gains.

For other families, no dollar infrastructure adjustment is published here without
a matched account-duration convention. Existing source balances are not silently
reinterpreted as comparable monthly profits.
