The low-value screens are named in a supplement but not as trial rows. `research/symbol_coverage.csv` lacks ENTR-CB-1's STOP. The Oct 19 scout report and SEEN rows are uncommitted.

## 9. New experiment 2: ATTN-1, attention-spike avoidance filter

ATTN-1 asks whether dropping coins whose Wikipedia attention just spiked improves the same Coinbase-tradable altcoin basket UNLOCK-1 uses. It costs nothing, its daily data is point-in-time, and it must beat both random removal and removal of the biggest recent price risers to show it adds anything. Its prior is weak and mixed. It runs in phases with a stop after each; nothing is downloaded before the registration and the coin-to-article mapping are frozen and reviewed. Result, Oct 21: Phase 0 ended BLOCKED\_DATA (gate a: 55.6% against 70%), so the specification below is kept only as a record (section 11).

| Field | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Hypothesis | In 4-week cycles of Coinbase-tradable top-100 altcoins, removing every eligible coin whose English Wikipedia article had a 7-day mean of daily user pageviews at least 3 times its trailing 90-day median, measured on days before selection, raises the basket's net return per $1,000 cycle by more than removing the same number of eligible coins at random or by largest recent price rise. |
| Rationale | Late buyers chase attention, then prices revert. Evidence is mixed (scout T06, CLAIMED from the sources): a 2023 preprint finds abnormal tweets before small-coin pump events go with later losses of about 1.3-2.2% per standard deviation over a month; a peer-reviewed paper finds no reversal after organic Twitter attention and a small positive next-day effect; our survey calls Wikipedia and Twitter attention "front-loaded or decayed". Most of the evidence is on coins smaller than the top 100. Wikipedia pageviews have never been used in any repo study. |
| Host basket | The Coinbase-tradable altcoin control from ENTR-CB-1: 147 coins, 4,209 selections, 100 four-week cycles 2019-2026; lower-bound mean +$17.16 per $1,000 cycle at 0.50% fees. The 40 most-held coins account for 2,726 of the 4,209 selections. Its per-coin returns were already seen; only the attention data would be new. |
| Data | Wikimedia per-article daily pageviews, user agent, free. Wikimedia added a separate "automated" agent type on 2020-04-29, so user counts before and after are not comparable. The primary sample is therefore cycles with selection on or after 2020-08-04, when a 90-day baseline lies wholly after the break: about 80 of the 100 cycles. Earlier cycles are a declared sensitivity. A daily count is published after the day ends, so there is no look-ahead beyond the mapping itself. |
| Phase 0: feasibility (no download) | Build a mapping from each of the 147 CMC IDs to one English Wikipedia article (or none), with a reason for each, and have it independently reviewed. All gates must pass or ATTN-1 ends as BLOCKED\_DATA: a) mapped coins cover at least 70% of the primary sample's selections; b) every mapped article is about the coin itself, not a company, person or disambiguation page; c) Wikimedia's terms allow research use, and the request budget is stated (about 150-300 requests); d) the user-agent series is available from 2020-04-29 on. |
| Eligibility | A coin is eligible in a cycle only if it is mapped, its article existed for the whole 97-day window before selection, at least 90% of those days have data, and its 90-day median is at least 30 daily views. Ineligible coins are never flagged and are excluded from both placebo pools. |
| Phase 1: method | For each cycle, flag an eligible coin if its spike ratio (7-day mean ending the day before selection, divided by the median of the 90 days before that) is at least 3. Filtered basket: equal weight across unflagged coins; cash if every coin is flagged. Same selection, entry, exit and valuation rules as the host, using the same engine. Paired difference per cycle, filtered minus unfiltered, on jointly valued cycles only. |
| Placebos | Random placebo: in each cycle, remove the same number of randomly chosen eligible coins; 2,000 draws, seed 20261022. Price-matched placebo: remove the same number of eligible coins with the largest change in CMC reference price from the snapshot 7 days before selection to the selection snapshot; ties broken by lower CMC ID; a coin missing either price is not eligible for this placebo. |
| Inference | 4-cycle block bootstrap, 4,000 resamples, seed 20261023, for the paired difference and for the attention-minus-price-matched difference. |
| Costs | Primary at the host's costs (0.50% fee plus 0.25% adverse price per side). Repeated at a 0.90% fee, which must not reverse the sign of the paired mean. |
| Metrics | Primary: mean paired difference per $1,000 cycle and its bootstrap 95% interval. Also: the same without the largest cycle; the random placebo's 95th percentile; the attention-minus-price-matched difference and its interval; share of selections removed; overlap between flagged coins and the top fifth by 7-day price rise; mean return of removed vs kept selections; worst cycle; compounded drawdown of both baskets. Three declared sensitivities, reported but never deciding: spike threshold 2, spike threshold 5, and including the pre-August-2020 cycles. |
| Success | Qualifies for forward confirmation only if all hold: bootstrap 95% lower bound of the paired mean above 0; paired mean above 0 without its largest cycle; paired mean above the random placebo's 95th percentile; bootstrap 95% lower bound of the attention-minus-price-matched difference above 0; paired mean still above 0 at the 0.90% fee; at most 30% of selections removed; all Phase 0 gates passed. Labelled exploratory, because host prices were already seen. |
| Failure | Paired mean at or below 0: STOP the attention filter on this host. A positive mean that misses any other condition: INCONCLUSIVE. No threshold tuning, no rerun. |
| Phase 2 (later, separate approval) | Apply the frozen filter forward to new cycles, with pageviews logged daily from registration. |
| Limitations | The host has no established edge, so a pass leaves no deployable strategy on its own; at a 0.90% fee the host's own mean would be lower (about +$9 per cycle by my estimate). Host prices were already seen. English Wikipedia attention is not crypto-native attention (X, Telegram). Newer coins are less likely to have articles. New York residents may not be able to trade every host coin (scout T10-9). |
| Cost | $0. Phase 0: about 1 working session plus the mapping review. Phase 1: about 150-300 free API requests, 2-3 sessions including tests, then an independent review; about 2-3 weeks to an exploratory result. |
| Approvals needed, in order | 1) Phase 0: mapping and documentation only. 2) Freeze of the registration and mapping after independent review. 3) Only then, the pageview download and its request budget. 4) Phase 2, later. |
| What each result changes | Pass: forward confirmation, and a separate decision on an altcoin sleeve using the filter. Fail: attention-spike avoidance closed for this host. Blocked: UNLOCK-1 or FRESH-1 becomes experiment 2. |
