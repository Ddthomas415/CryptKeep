5a. Experiment 1: E1, maker vs taker execution cost
E1 asks whether a resting limit order at the touch, with a market-order fallback after 60 minutes, beats an immediate market order by more than 10 bps per side for BTC-USD and ETH-USD at the candidate's rebalance time. It follows the existing draft, maker_taker_prereg_20261011/PREREGISTRATION_draft_v3.md, without changing its constants.
Field
Specification
Hypothesis
At 00:00 UTC, a post-only limit order at the best bid (buy) or best ask (sell), left for 60 minutes and then completed with a market order, has lower all-in cost than an immediate market order of the same size, by more than 10 bps per side.
Rationale
The candidate's registered verdict is REJECT at 1.2% per side and keep_researching at 0.50%, so cost decides its fate. At its measured turnover of 9-13 times capital a year, each 10 bps per side is worth about 0.9-1.3 points a year; the maximum saving of 25 bps is about 2.3-3.2% of capital a year before missed fills. Separately, slippage has been assumed (0.05% to 0.25% per side across studies) and never measured; E1 measures BTC and ETH market-order cost at the real decision time.
Data
Coinbase public order book (Advanced Trade product book) at T0 = 00:00:30 UTC, at arrival A = T0 + 2 seconds, and at T1 = T0 + 60 minutes. Coinbase public trades with complete trade-ID coverage of the window. Product size and price increments. Product-specific fees from your dated screenshot. All public: no keys, no orders. All new forward data.
Method
Every day, 4 events: BTC and ETH, buy and sell, each sized q = $500 / mid-price at T0, rounded down to the product increment. Market baseline: walk the arrival book for q. Limit method: price at the T0 touch; fills under three scenarios (primary: a later trade through the limit price; secondary: queue-adjusted; optimistic: any trade at the limit); unfilled remainder completed at T1 with the taker fee. Cost per side in bps against the T0 mid, fees included. 3 validation days discarded, then a fixed 56-day sample (at most 224 events). Inference: circular day-block bootstrap, 10,000 resamples, 7-day blocks (3 and 14 as sensitivities), seed 20261011.
Baseline
Immediate market order at the same arrival time, same quantity, same reference price.
Metrics
Primary: mean D = limit-method cost minus market cost, bps per side, with 95% interval. Also: fill fraction per scenario; 95th-percentile limit-method cost; results by asset and side; exclusions and reasons; a modelled post-fill drift check for adverse selection; measured market-order cost against the assumed slippage. Any whole-strategy saving is computed separately on the candidate's actual weekly trades, never extrapolated from the daily panel.
Success
MAKER SUPPORTED: upper end of the 95% interval below -10 bps in the primary scenario, with at most 10% of events excluded.
Failure
MAKER NOT WORTH IT: lower end of the 95% interval above -10 bps. Anything else is INCONCLUSIVE. DEGRADED takes precedence: more than 10% exclusions is reported as DEGRADED, whatever the interval says, and changes nothing. No extensions or reruns.
Your advisor's five checks
Fee correctness: a gate before freeze. Fill rate: modelled under three scenarios only. Adverse selection: modelled post-fill drift, not realized. Missed fills: included, because the unfilled part is completed at T1 prices. Whole-strategy effect: computed on the candidate's actual weekly trades.
Open items before freeze
The arrival freshness tolerance is unset in draft v3. 56 days in 7-day blocks gives about 8 blocks, so intervals may be too narrow; the freeze reviewer must decide whether to keep that constant. Handling of empty resamples is an open clause.
Limitations
No real orders, so true fill probability and queue position stay unknown. 56 days covers one market regime. The balanced daily panel is not the strategy's real order flow. Model orders of $500 are 20 times the live $25-per-trade gate, so at live size the saving is cents per trade. A machine must be awake at 00:00 UTC every day; missed days count as exclusions.
Cost
$0 spend. Build: a collector with time-aligned order-book capture, an analysis script, tests and an independent review, about 3-4 working sessions (estimate). Calendar: 3 + 56 + about 7 days, about 66 days.
Approvals needed
1) Dated screenshot of BTC-USD and ETH-USD maker and taker fees. 2) Approval of the arrival order-book capture rule and its freshness tolerance (draft v3 section 4). 3) Where the collector runs: your Mac, awake from about 7:00 to 8:01 pm New York time (EST after Nov 1), or the server, which is a separate operational change. 4) Freeze after independent review. 5) Optional, not proposed now: tiny real limit orders to observe actual fills, which is live trading.
What each result changes
Supported: a separately approved second paper ledger using limit orders; the frozen weekly test is untouched. Not worth it: keep market orders and replace the assumed slippage with the measured cost. Inconclusive or degraded: no change.
5b. Experiment 2: UNLOCK-1, token-unlock avoidance filter
UNLOCK-1 asks whether dropping coins that face a large scheduled team or investor unlock improves a Coinbase-tradable altcoin basket. It is a risk filter, not a buy signal. It runs in phases with a stop after each, and its free feasibility check may well end it: the one vendor checked keeps only a single revision date per schedule.
Field
Specification
Hypothesis
In 4-week cycles of Coinbase-tradable top-100 altcoins, removing every coin with a scheduled team or investor unlock of at least 1% of its circulating supply, dated from entry to 30 days after exit, raises the basket's net return per $1,000 cycle by more than removing the same number of coins at random.
Rationale
New supply plus holders hedging ahead of it. Keyrock, a market maker that sells unlock-related services, reports negative price pressure in 90% of 16,000+ unlock events across 40 tokens, often starting 30 days before. That averages about 400 events per token, so most were small or linear. It found team unlocks the worst, investor unlocks "controlled" and ecosystem unlocks positive (+1.18%), normalised prices against ETH, and gave no significance or survivorship tests. Our survey ranked supply-event avoidance third. Unlock schedules have never been used in any repo study.
Host basket
The Coinbase-tradable altcoin control from ENTR-CB-1: all eligible top-100 altcoins listed on Coinbase at entry, equal weight, 100 four-week cycles 2019-2026, 4,209 selections; lower-bound mean +$17.16 per $1,000 cycle, median -$23.38, worst cycle -$419.82. Its per-coin returns were already seen in ENTR-CB-1 and the low-value screens; only the unlock data would be new.
Data
Unlock schedules: DefiLlama's Pro API (/api/emissions, /api/emission/{protocol}; $300 a month; one lastModified date per protocol). Other vendors not checked. Prices, selections and listing dates: already in the repo. Circulating supply derived as market cap divided by price from each CMC snapshot. No new price downloads.
Phase 0: feasibility (no spend)
Public documentation and any free sample only; no API calls without your yes. All five gates must pass or UNLOCK-1 ends as BLOCKED_DATA with nothing spent. a) Coverage: at least 70% of the 4,209 selections belong to tokens the source tracks (a tracked token with no unlock in the window counts as covered; an untracked token does not). b) Point-in-time: the source shows when each schedule entry was published or revised, so schedules can be rebuilt as known at each selection. c) Dead and delisted tokens are included. d) Recipient type is given. e) The licence allows research use. Likely outcome with DefiLlama: gate b) fails.
Phase 1: method
Registration frozen before any unlock data is pulled. Flag a selected coin if a team or investor unlock of at least 1% of circulating supply is dated from entry to 30 days after exit, using only schedule versions dated on or before the selection date. Filtered basket: equal weight across unflagged coins; cash when every coin is flagged. Same selection, entry, exit, costs and valuation rules as the host, using the same engine. Paired difference per cycle, filtered minus unfiltered, on jointly valued cycles only (no unresolved exit or missing entry in either basket).
Inference
4-cycle block bootstrap, 4,000 resamples, seed 20261020. Placebo: in each cycle, remove the same number of randomly chosen coins as the filter removed, 2,000 draws, seed 20261021.
Baseline
The unfiltered host basket and the random-removal placebo, on the same cycles. Hold BTC, the BTC/ETH/cash mix, cash and the candidate are reported for context.
Metrics
Primary: mean paired difference per $1,000 cycle and its bootstrap 95% interval. Also: the same with the largest cycle removed; the placebo's 95th percentile; share of selections removed; mean return of removed vs kept selections; worst cycle; compounded drawdown of both baskets; turnover change. Two declared sensitivities, reported but never deciding: unlocks of any recipient type, and a 0.5% threshold.
Success
Qualifies for forward confirmation only if all hold: bootstrap 95% lower bound of the paired mean above 0; paired mean above 0 without its largest cycle; paired mean above the placebo's 95th percentile; no more than 30% of selections removed; all Phase 0 gates passed. Labelled exploratory, because host prices were already seen.
Failure
Paired mean at or below 0: STOP the unlock filter on this host, record it, no threshold tuning. A positive mean that misses any other success condition: INCONCLUSIVE, no rerun.
Phase 2 (later, separate approval)
Log every large scheduled unlock on Coinbase-tradable coins from registration onward, and apply the frozen filter to new cycles.
Limitations
The host has no established edge, so a pass improves the basket against itself and leaves no deployable strategy on its own. Host prices were already seen. An unlock is not a sale. Unlocks may cluster with market-wide moves. Forward confirmation is slow: about 13 cycles a year.
Cost
Phase 0: about 1 working session, $0. If it passes and you approve: $300 for one month of data, 2-3 sessions for Phase 1 including tests, then an independent review; about 2-3 weeks to an exploratory result.
Approvals needed
1) Phase 0 using public documentation only (any vendor API call needs your yes). 2) The $300 spend and the new data source, only after Phase 0 passes. 3) Freeze after independent review. 4) Phase 2, later.
What each result changes
Pass: forward confirmation, and a separate decision on whether to design an altcoin sleeve with this filter. Fail: supply-event avoidance closed for this host. Blocked: FUND-1 becomes experiment 2 and I write its full spec.
